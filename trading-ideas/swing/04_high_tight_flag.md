# 04 — High Tight Flag

## Overview
Trade a powerful continuation pattern where a stock doubles or rallies sharply, then consolidates tightly instead of giving back much ground.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock advances at least 40%-80% in 4-8 weeks.
- Consolidation lasts 2-5 weeks and retraces less than 25%-35%.
- Volume contracts during the flag.
- Enter on close above flag high with volume expansion.
- Stop below flag midpoint or recent swing low.

## Exit Rules
- Take partial at 2R.
- Trail with 10-day or 20-day MA.
- Exit on failed breakout back into flag.

## Filters
- Require strong earnings, sector, or thematic support.
- Avoid if the move is only illiquid speculation.
- Best in strong bull markets.

## Risk Management
Use smaller size because volatility is high. Never chase more than 5% above breakout.
