# 64 — Defensive Rotation Swing

## Overview
Buy defensive stocks when market breadth weakens and defensive sectors start outperforming.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Index shows distribution or breaks 50-day MA.
- Defensive sector relative strength turns up.
- Candidate stock forms a base or reclaims 20-day MA.
- Enter on breakout above short-term resistance.
- Stop below base low.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R or prior weekly resistance.
- Exit if risk-on sectors regain leadership.
- Trail with 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Prefer stable earnings and dividends.
- Avoid illiquid defensive names.
- Confirm sector breadth.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-0.75%. Defensive trades are lower beta but not risk-free.
