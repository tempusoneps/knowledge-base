# 75 — Micro Pullback Trend Scalp

## Overview

Join a strong intraday trend using very small pullbacks instead of chasing the impulse candle. The setup works best on trend days where waiting for a deep retracement means missing the move.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 1-min execution, 5-min trend filter |
| Markets | VN30F, liquid large-cap stocks |
| Context | VWAP trending and price holding one side of it |

### Long Entry
1. 5-min structure shows higher highs and higher lows.
2. Price is above rising VWAP and 9-EMA.
3. A 1-min pullback lasts 2-4 candles and volume contracts.
4. Enter when price breaks the pullback high.
5. Stop below the pullback low.

### Short Entry
1. 5-min structure shows lower highs and lower lows.
2. Price is below falling VWAP and 9-EMA.
3. A 1-min bounce lasts 2-4 candles and volume contracts.
4. Enter when price breaks the pullback low.
5. Stop above the pullback high.

## Exit Rules

- Take profit at 1R-1.5R for scalps.
- Trail one candle behind if the move accelerates.
- Stop trading the setup after two failed pullbacks.

## Filters

- Avoid range days where VWAP is flat.
- Prefer opening drive or afternoon expansion.
- Skip pullbacks that retrace more than 50% of the prior impulse.

## Risk Management

Risk 0.25%-0.5% per scalp. The edge is frequency plus tight invalidation, not large targets.
