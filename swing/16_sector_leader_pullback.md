# 16 — Sector Leader Pullback

## Overview
Buy the strongest stock in a leading sector when it pulls back in an orderly way while the sector trend remains intact.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Sector outperforms the market over 1-3 months.
- Stock is among top relative-strength names in the sector.
- Pullback reaches 20-day or 50-day MA on declining volume.
- Enter on bullish reversal or break of pullback trendline.
- Stop below pullback low.

## Exit Rules
- Target prior high first.
- Trail with 20-day MA if new highs continue.
- Exit if sector loses leadership.

## Filters
- Avoid laggards when leaders are available.
- Prefer improving fundamentals.
- Skip if broad market enters distribution.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Concentrate on best-in-sector, not many similar names.
