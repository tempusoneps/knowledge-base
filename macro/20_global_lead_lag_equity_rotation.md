# 20 — Global Lead-Lag Equity Rotation

## Overview
Rotate into a local market when global lead markets turn up and local price begins confirming.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Major global index breaks above 50-day or 200-day MA.
- Local market stops underperforming.
- Currency and credit conditions are stable.
- Enter local index/sector leaders on breakout.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if global lead market fails breakout.
- Stop if local relative strength rolls over.

## Filters
- Avoid markets with domestic policy shock.
- Prefer local sectors aligned with global leadership.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%-1.5%. Global signals help timing but local risk controls trade outcome.
