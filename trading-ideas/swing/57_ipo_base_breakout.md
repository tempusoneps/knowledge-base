# 57 — IPO Base Breakout

## Overview
Buy a recently listed stock after it forms its first tradable base and breaks out with volume.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock has traded at least 4-8 weeks.
- IPO volatility settles into a definable base.
- Price holds above IPO low and relative strength improves.
- Enter on breakout above base high.
- Stop below breakout day low or base midpoint.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R.
- Exit if price closes back inside base.
- Trail with 10-day MA if momentum expands.

## Filters
- Prefer strong revenue growth or sector theme.
- Avoid lockup-expiration risk windows.
- Require adequate liquidity.

## Risk Management
Use smaller size because IPOs have limited trading history.
