# 23 — Failed Breakdown at Weekly Support

## Overview
Buy when price breaks a weekly support level, traps sellers, and quickly reclaims the level.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Weekly support is visible from multiple prior touches.
- Price breaks below support but closes back above it within 1-3 sessions.
- Volume on breakdown does not follow through.
- Enter on daily close back above support.
- Stop below failed breakdown low.

## Exit Rules
- Target range midpoint first.
- Second target: top of the weekly range.
- Exit if weekly close fails back below support.

## Filters
- Avoid if breakdown follows major bad news.
- Best when market is recovering from oversold conditions.
- Prefer strong balance sheets or sector leaders.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-1%. The setup is invalid if reclaimed support fails.
