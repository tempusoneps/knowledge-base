# 14 — Acquirer vs Target Merger Pair

## Overview
Trade announced stock-for-stock mergers by hedging target shares against acquirer shares according to deal ratio.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Confirm deal terms and exchange ratio.
- Long target and short acquirer if spread is wide and deal probability is acceptable.
- Enter after liquidity stabilizes post-announcement.
- Size by exchange ratio.

## Exit Rules
- Exit when spread narrows to target return.
- Stop on adverse regulatory, financing, or shareholder news.

## Filters
- Avoid deals with unclear terms.
- Adjust for dividends and expected closing date.

## Risk Management
Deal breaks can gap violently. Use small size and monitor news.
