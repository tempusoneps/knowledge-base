# 20 — Valuation Band Trend Hold

## Overview
Hold a stock while valuation is reasonable relative to growth and price remains in a long-term uptrend.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Company grows earnings or cash flow consistently.
- Valuation sits within historical fair band.
- Price is above 30-week MA and relative strength is stable.
- Enter on weekly pullback or breakout from fair-value consolidation.

## Exit Rules
- Trim when valuation reaches extreme band and price becomes extended.
- Exit if growth slows and price breaks 30-week MA.

## Filters
- Avoid using valuation alone as timing.
- Prefer companies with visible earnings runway.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%-1.5%. Combine fundamental review with technical stops.
