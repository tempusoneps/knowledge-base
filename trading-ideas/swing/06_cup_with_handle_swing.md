# 06 — Cup with Handle Swing

## Overview
Buy a rounded base followed by a shallow handle, aiming to catch the next uptrend leg.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Cup forms over 7-30 weeks with a rounded bottom.
- Handle forms in the upper half of the cup.
- Handle volume contracts and shakeout is limited.
- Enter on close above handle high.
- Stop below handle low.

## Exit Rules
- Target measured move from handle breakout or 2R-3R.
- Exit if price closes back below handle low.
- Trail below 10-week MA for larger winners.

## Filters
- Avoid V-shaped cups with no consolidation.
- Prefer positive earnings revisions or sector leadership.
- Skip if handle forms below declining 200-day MA.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-1%. Handles should be shallow; wide handles reduce expectancy.
