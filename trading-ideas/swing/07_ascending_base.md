# 07 — Ascending Base

## Overview
Buy a stock that forms a series of higher lows under a stubborn resistance area, showing steady accumulation.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Resistance is tested at least 3 times over several weeks.
- Each pullback forms a higher low.
- Volume is quiet on pullbacks and improves on advances.
- Enter on daily close above resistance.
- Stop below the last higher low.

## Exit Rules
- Target measured move from base height.
- Exit if price returns below resistance and cannot reclaim.
- Trail with rising 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Prefer rising relative strength line.
- Avoid if base sits below major weekly resistance.
- Better when sector breadth is improving.

## Risk Management
Risk no more than 1%. Skip if last higher low is too far from entry.
