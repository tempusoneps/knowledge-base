# 65 — High-Dividend Support Swing

## Overview
Buy high-dividend stocks when yield support aligns with technical reversal, aiming for a lower-volatility swing.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Dividend yield is attractive relative to its history.
- Price tests multi-month support.
- Reversal candle forms with improving volume.
- Enter above reversal high.
- Stop below support low.

## Exit Rules
- Target range high or 2R.
- Exit before ex-dividend if price already captured expected move.
- Exit if support breaks.

## Filters
- Avoid dividend traps with falling earnings.
- Check ex-dividend date and payout sustainability.
- Prefer stable balance sheets.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-0.75%. Dividend yield should not justify ignoring stops.
