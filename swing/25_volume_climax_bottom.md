# 25 — Volume Climax Bottom

## Overview
Buy a capitulation low only after the climax day is followed by price stabilization and a reclaim trigger.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock falls sharply into support on volume above 2x average.
- Climax candle has a long lower wick or strong close off lows.
- Next 1-3 sessions do not break the climax low.
- Enter above the stabilization range high.
- Stop below climax low.

## Exit Rules
- Target 20-day MA or prior breakdown level.
- Exit if climax low fails.
- Take partials into first resistance.

## Filters
- Avoid if climax is due to confirmed insolvency/fraud/delisting risk.
- Better in index-wide capitulation.
- Prefer liquid stocks.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%. Capitulation trades are tactical and must not become investments.
