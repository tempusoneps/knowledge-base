# 29 — Accumulation Days Cluster

## Overview
Buy when a stock shows multiple institutional accumulation days in a short period while building a base.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- At least 3 up-volume days in 2-4 weeks.
- Price holds above 50-day MA or reclaims it.
- Down days show lower volume than up days.
- Enter on break of short-term resistance.
- Stop below recent higher low.

## Exit Rules
- Target weekly resistance or 2R-3R.
- Exit if distribution days appear and support breaks.
- Trail below 20-day MA after breakout.

## Filters
- Prefer stocks with improving fundamentals.
- Avoid if accumulation occurs below heavy overhead supply.
- Confirm sector participation.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Accumulation improves odds but does not remove stop risk.
