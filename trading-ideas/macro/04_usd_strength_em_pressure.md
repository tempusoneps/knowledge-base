# 04 — USD Strength EM Pressure

## Overview
Reduce or short EM-sensitive assets when USD strength and tighter liquidity pressure risk assets.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- USD index or relevant FX pair breaks above 3-month resistance.
- EM equities or local index underperforms global benchmark.
- Enter short/underweight EM basket or hedge with futures.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if USD loses breakout.
- Cover if EM relative strength reclaims 50-day MA.

## Filters
- Confirm local rates/FX reserves context.
- Avoid if domestic policy offsets USD pressure.

## Risk Management
Use hedges and position caps; FX-led moves can gap on central-bank action.
