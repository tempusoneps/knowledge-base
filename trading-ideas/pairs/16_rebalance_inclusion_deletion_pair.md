# 16 — Inclusion vs Deletion Rebalance Pair

## Overview
Long index inclusion candidates and short deletion candidates around rebalance flow.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Confirm index rebalance announcement.
- Long additions or weight increases.
- Short deletions or weight decreases where possible.
- Enter after initial reaction holds and ratio confirms.

## Exit Rules
- Exit before or on effective date unless flow persists.
- Stop if announcement details change.

## Filters
- Use names with meaningful passive-flow impact.
- Avoid crowded trades already fully priced.

## Risk Management
Event flow reverses quickly after effective date. Reduce early into crowded moves.
