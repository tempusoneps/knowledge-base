# 09 — Sector Momentum Rotation

## Overview
Allocate to sectors with the strongest intermediate-term momentum.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Rank sectors by 3-month and 6-month returns.
- Buy top 2-3 sectors above 200-day MA.
- Rebalance monthly.
- Hold cash/defensive asset if no sector is above trend.

## Exit Rules
- Exit sector when rank drops below top half or trend breaks.
- Rebalance on schedule.

## Filters
- Use liquidity-adjusted sector baskets.
- Avoid overconcentration in highly correlated sectors.

## Risk Management
Equal-volatility sector weights and max drawdown rule.
