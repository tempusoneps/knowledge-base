# 37 — Prior Low Retest Rejection

## Overview
Sell or short-watch when a broken major support level becomes resistance on the rebound.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock breaks below a multi-month support.
- Rebound retests the old support from below.
- Volume fades on the rebound.
- Enter short where available, or sell longs, below rejection candle.
- Stop above retest high.

## Exit Rules
- Target prior breakdown low.
- Second target: next weekly support.
- Exit if price reclaims old support for 2 sessions.

## Filters
- Stronger if 50-day and 200-day MAs slope down.
- Avoid if breakdown was caused by temporary market panic only.
- Confirm weak sector.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-0.75%. Broken support can reclaim violently in bear traps.
