# 10 — PMI Reacceleration Cyclicals

## Overview
Buy cyclicals when PMI or equivalent activity data turns up and price confirms.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- PMI rises for 2-3 consecutive readings or crosses above 50.
- Cyclical sectors outperform defensives.
- Enter industrial/materials basket on weekly breakout.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if PMI rolls over for two readings.
- Stop if cyclical/defensive ratio fails.

## Filters
- Avoid if central-bank tightening overwhelms growth signal.
- Confirm earnings revisions.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%. Economic data is lagged; price confirmation is mandatory.
