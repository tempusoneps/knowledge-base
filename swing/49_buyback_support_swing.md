# 49 — Buyback Support Swing

## Overview
Buy a stock after a credible buyback announcement if price forms support and starts to trend higher.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Company announces meaningful buyback relative to market cap and liquidity.
- Price holds above announcement day low.
- Pullback volume declines.
- Enter on break above post-announcement consolidation high.
- Stop below consolidation low.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R or prior weekly resistance.
- Exit if announcement day low fails.
- Trail with 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Prefer companies with cash to execute buyback.
- Avoid symbolic buybacks too small to matter.
- Confirm no major negative earnings revision.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Buybacks support demand but do not guarantee trend.
