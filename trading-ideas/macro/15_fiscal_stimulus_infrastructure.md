# 15 — Fiscal Stimulus Infrastructure

## Overview
Buy infrastructure and construction beneficiaries when fiscal spending is confirmed and price breaks out.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Fiscal package or public investment acceleration is official.
- Infrastructure basket relative strength turns up.
- Enter leaders on weekly breakout or first pullback.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if spending implementation delays materially.
- Stop if basket loses breakout level.

## Filters
- Prefer companies with direct project exposure.
- Avoid firms with margin pressure or poor execution history.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%. Policy implementation timing can lag headlines.
