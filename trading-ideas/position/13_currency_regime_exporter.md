# 13 — Currency Regime Exporter

## Overview
Hold exporters that benefit from a sustained currency trend.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Home currency weakens in a multi-month trend.
- Exporter margins or revenue translation should benefit.
- Stock breaks a weekly base and outperforms domestic index.
- Enter on breakout or 10-week MA pullback.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if currency trend reverses for a month.
- Exit if company hedging neutralizes expected benefit.

## Filters
- Confirm export revenue mix.
- Avoid firms with imported input costs that offset FX benefit.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%. FX-driven position trades can reverse on policy intervention.
