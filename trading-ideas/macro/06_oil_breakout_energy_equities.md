# 06 — Oil Breakout Energy Equities

## Overview
Buy energy equities when oil breaks a major range and producers confirm.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Oil closes above 3-6 month resistance.
- Energy equity/oil ratio is stable or rising.
- Enter energy leaders on weekly breakout or pullback.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if oil returns into old range.
- Stop if energy sector loses 50-day MA.

## Filters
- Confirm producers benefit from spot prices.
- Avoid if refining margins contradict the trade.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%. Commodity volatility requires wider stops and smaller size.
