# 20 — Market Breadth Thrust Swing

## Overview
Buy high-quality setups after a broad market breadth thrust indicates risk appetite has returned.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Market breadth surges: strong advancers/decliners or percent above 20-day MA.
- Index reclaims 50-day MA or breaks a downtrend.
- Candidate stock is in a leading sector and near a breakout level.
- Enter on candidate breakout or first pullback after breadth thrust.
- Stop below setup low.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R.
- Exit if market breadth thrust fully reverses.
- Trail winning positions while index stays above 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Avoid low-quality laggards.
- Stronger after oversold corrections.
- Confirm with volume follow-through.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Add exposure gradually, not all on the first thrust day.
