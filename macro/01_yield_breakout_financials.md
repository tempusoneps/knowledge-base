# 01 — Yield Breakout Financials

## Overview
Trade financial stocks or ETFs when bond yields break higher and banks confirm with relative strength.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- 10-year yield closes above a 3-month range.
- Yield curve is stable or steepening.
- Financial sector relative strength breaks out.
- Enter financial ETF/leader on weekly breakout.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if yields fall back into range.
- Stop if financial sector loses breakout.

## Filters
- Avoid if credit stress rises sharply.
- Prefer banks with asset-sensitive balance sheets.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1.5%. Macro confirmation must align with sector price action.
