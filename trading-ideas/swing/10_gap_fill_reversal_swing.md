# 10 — Gap Fill Reversal Swing

## Overview
Buy or sell after a gap is fully filled and price rejects the pre-gap level, often marking the end of a corrective move.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Identify a prior breakaway or earnings gap.
- Price returns to fill the gap over days or weeks.
- Volume declines into the fill zone.
- Enter long after bullish reversal from bullish gap fill; enter short after bearish rejection from bearish gap fill.
- Stop beyond the gap-fill rejection low/high.

## Exit Rules
- First target: gap midpoint.
- Second target: far side of gap or prior swing.
- Exit if price accepts beyond the filled gap.

## Filters
- Best when original gap had real catalyst.
- Avoid if new negative/positive news changes the thesis.
- Confirm with market trend.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-1%. Treat the gap level as a zone, not an exact tick.
