# 09 — Credit Spread Widening Defense

## Overview
Shift defensive or hedge equity exposure when credit spreads widen and equities have not fully priced stress.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- High-yield or local credit spreads break above 3-month range.
- Equity index loses 50-day MA or breadth deteriorates.
- Enter defensive basket, reduce beta, or add index hedge.

## Exit Rules
- Remove hedge when spreads fall back into range.
- Re-risk when breadth improves.

## Filters
- Avoid false signals from illiquid credit data.
- Confirm with bank and small-cap weakness.

## Risk Management
This is portfolio protection. Define hedge size before stress accelerates.
