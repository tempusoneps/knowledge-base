# 56 — Float Rotation Exhaustion

## Overview
Sell or fade a speculative swing after turnover suggests most of the float has changed hands and price stops advancing.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock rallies sharply over several days/weeks.
- Cumulative volume approaches or exceeds free float.
- Price makes marginal new highs with weaker closes.
- Enter short where available, or exit longs, below reversal day low.
- Stop above exhaustion high.

## Exit Rules
- Target 10-day or 20-day MA.
- Exit if price reclaims exhaustion high.
- Take profits quickly in volatile names.

## Filters
- Best in low-float speculative runners.
- Avoid if a major fundamental re-rating is real.
- Confirm with volume climax.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%. Exhaustion shorts can squeeze; keep stops hard.
