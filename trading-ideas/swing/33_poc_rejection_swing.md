# 33 — Multi-Week POC Rejection

## Overview
Fade or exit when price rallies into a multi-week point of control and gets rejected, showing fair value has become resistance.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Identify point of control from recent 3-6 month range.
- Price approaches POC from below after a decline.
- Rejection candle forms with weak close or high volume.
- Enter short where available, or sell longs, below rejection low.
- Stop above rejection high.

## Exit Rules
- Target lower value area or recent swing low.
- Exit if price closes above POC for 2 sessions.
- Take partial at 1.5R-2R.

## Filters
- Stronger when 50-day MA is falling.
- Avoid if stock has fresh bullish catalyst.
- Confirm broad market weakness.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%. POC is a zone, so avoid entries with poor R:R.
