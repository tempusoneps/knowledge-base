# 50 — Insider Buy Cluster

## Overview
Buy after multiple insiders or major holders purchase shares, but only when price confirms accumulation.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Insider/major-holder purchases are disclosed.
- Buying is meaningful relative to historical activity.
- Stock forms a base or reclaims 50-day MA.
- Enter on breakout above post-disclosure range.
- Stop below range low.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R or next weekly resistance.
- Exit if price loses the base.
- Use time stop after 6-8 weeks if no progress.

## Filters
- Ignore tiny token purchases.
- Stronger when purchases follow a large decline.
- Confirm liquidity.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-1%. Insider buying is supportive, not a standalone entry.
