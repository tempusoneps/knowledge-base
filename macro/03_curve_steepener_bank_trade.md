# 03 — Yield Curve Steepener Bank Trade

## Overview
Trade banks when the yield curve steepens after a period of compression.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- 2s10s or local curve spread breaks above 3-month average.
- Bank sector outperforms market for at least 2 weeks.
- Enter bank basket/leader on pullback or breakout.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if curve flattens back below trigger.
- Stop if bank basket loses 50-day MA.

## Filters
- Avoid if steepening is caused by default-risk panic.
- Confirm credit spreads are not blowing out.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%. Curve trades need credit-quality checks.
