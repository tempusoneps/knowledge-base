# 63 — Market Regime Filter Breakout

## Overview
Take breakout trades only when the market regime supports risk, reducing false breakouts in weak markets.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Index is above rising 50-day and 200-day MAs.
- Market breadth is improving.
- Candidate forms a valid base.
- Enter on breakout with volume confirmation.
- Stop below breakout level.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R.
- Exit if candidate fails or market regime turns defensive.
- Trail with 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Avoid breakouts when index is below falling 50-day MA.
- Prefer sectors leading the regime.
- Check distribution days.

## Risk Management
Risk 1% max in healthy regimes; reduce to 0.5% in mixed regimes.
