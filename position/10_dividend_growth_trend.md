# 10 — Dividend Growth Trend

## Overview
Own stocks with sustainable dividend growth while price remains in a long-term uptrend.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Dividend per share grows consistently.
- Payout ratio is sustainable.
- Price is above rising 30-week MA.
- Enter on pullback to weekly support or breakout from consolidation.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if dividend growth stops due to earnings weakness.
- Technical exit on monthly close below 10-month MA.

## Filters
- Avoid high yield caused by price collapse.
- Prefer stable free cash flow.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%-1.5%. Do not let yield override price and balance-sheet risk.
