# 18 — Capital Structure Equity Pair

## Overview
Pair a leveraged equity against a stronger balance-sheet peer when credit conditions change.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Identify same-sector high-leverage and low-leverage stocks.
- Credit spreads widen: long low-leverage, short high-leverage.
- Credit spreads tighten: consider reverse only with price confirmation.
- Enter on ratio breakout.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if credit regime reverses.
- Stop if ratio fails breakout.

## Filters
- Confirm debt maturity schedule and refinancing risk.
- Avoid companies with idiosyncratic litigation or asset sale news.

## Risk Management
Leveraged equities can gap; use smaller short leg and basket if possible.
