# 52 — Short Interest Squeeze Base

## Overview
Buy a heavily shorted stock only after it builds a base and breaks resistance, creating conditions for a controlled squeeze.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Short interest or bearish positioning is elevated.
- Stock stops making lower lows and forms a 4-8 week base.
- Breakout occurs above base resistance with strong volume.
- Enter on breakout or first retest hold.
- Stop below breakout level or base midpoint.

## Exit Rules
- Take partial into vertical extension.
- Trail with 10-day MA.
- Exit if breakout fails back into base.

## Filters
- Avoid weak companies with valid bearish thesis.
- Stronger with positive catalyst.
- Ensure borrow/short data is reliable.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%. Squeeze names are volatile; avoid full-size chasing.
