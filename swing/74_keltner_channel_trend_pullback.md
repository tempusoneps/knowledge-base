# 74 — Keltner Channel Trend Pullback

## Overview
Buy pullbacks toward the Keltner midline during a strong channel uptrend.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Price rides upper half of Keltner Channel.
- 20-day EMA slopes upward.
- Pullback reaches midline without closing below lower band.
- Enter when price closes back above prior day's high.
- Stop below pullback low.

## Exit Rules
- Target upper band retest.
- Trail below 20-day EMA.
- Exit if price closes below lower band.

## Filters
- Avoid flat channels.
- Prefer stocks with strong relative strength.
- Skip near earnings if gap risk is unacceptable.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Channel pullbacks work only when trend remains intact.
