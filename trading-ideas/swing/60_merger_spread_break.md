# 60 — Merger Spread Break

## Overview
Trade directional movement when a merger spread breaks out of its expected range due to changing deal probability.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Announced deal creates defined spread to offer price.
- Spread narrows for long setup or widens for sell/short setup.
- Price breaks a multi-week spread range with volume.
- Enter in direction of probability repricing.
- Stop beyond the broken spread range.

## Exit Rules
- Target next spread level or 2R.
- Exit if new deal news contradicts the move.
- Do not hold through binary legal/regulatory dates unless planned.

## Filters
- Requires understanding deal terms.
- Avoid illiquid deals.
- Monitor regulatory and financing risk.

## Risk Management
Use small size. Deal trades have binary gap risk.
