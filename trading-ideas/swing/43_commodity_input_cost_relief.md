# 43 — Commodity Input Cost Relief

## Overview
Buy companies whose margins improve when key input commodities fall, after the stock confirms a trend change.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Input commodity breaks down over several weeks.
- Candidate's sector begins outperforming.
- Stock reclaims 50-day MA or breaks a downtrend line.
- Enter on first constructive pullback after reclaim.
- Stop below reclaim low.

## Exit Rules
- Target prior swing high or 2R.
- Exit if commodity rebounds above breakdown level.
- Trail with 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Verify commodity is a major input cost.
- Avoid companies with demand problems larger than cost relief.
- Prefer improving earnings revisions.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%. Do not buy only because input prices fall; require price confirmation.
