# 11 — Earnings Gap and Go

## Overview
Buy an earnings gap that keeps advancing because the market is repricing future growth, not just reacting to one good quarter.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Earnings beat includes revenue, margin, or guidance improvement.
- Stock gaps above recent range on volume above 2x average.
- First 1-3 days hold above gap low.
- Enter on tight consolidation breakout above the gap-day high.
- Stop below consolidation low.

## Exit Rules
- Take partial at 2R.
- Hold runner while price stays above 10-day MA.
- Exit if gap low fails.

## Filters
- Prefer positive surprise plus strong sector.
- Avoid one-time gains with weak core business.
- Skip if liquidity is poor.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Earnings gaps are volatile; avoid oversized positions.
