# 48 — Index Deletion Pressure

## Overview
Sell or short-watch stocks expected to leave an index, using breakdown confirmation to avoid premature bearish trades.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Deletion or weight cut is announced or highly probable.
- Stock fails to reclaim 20-day or 50-day MA.
- Volume expands on down days.
- Enter short where available, or exit longs, on support break.
- Stop above broken support or 20-day MA.

## Exit Rules
- Target next weekly support.
- Cover/exit before effective date if crowded.
- Exit if stock reclaims broken support.

## Filters
- Avoid if fundamentals are improving enough to offset flow.
- Confirm actual index impact.
- Prefer liquid names.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-0.75%. Deletion trades can reverse after forced selling ends.
