# 72 — 55-Day Donchian Breakout

## Overview
Trade a medium-term trend breakout using a 55-day high or low as a systematic trigger.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Long: price closes above 55-day high and above 200-day MA.
- Short/sell: price closes below 55-day low and below 200-day MA.
- ATR is not excessively expanded versus recent history.
- Enter on breakout close or controlled retest.
- Stop at 20-day low/high or 2 ATR.

## Exit Rules
- Trail using 20-day low for longs or 20-day high for shorts.
- Exit if trend reverses through trailing level.
- Take partial at 3R if discretionary.

## Filters
- Works best in trending markets.
- Avoid choppy indexes.
- Confirm liquidity.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-1%. Expect many small losses and occasional large winners.
