# 76 — ATR Percentile Expansion Breakout

## Overview

Trade the first clean breakout after intraday volatility expands from an unusually quiet state. The signal is not just price range compression; it requires current ATR to move from a low percentile into expansion.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 5-min chart |
| Markets | VN30F, liquid stocks |
| Indicator | 5-min ATR compared with last 20 sessions |

### Long Entry
1. Morning ATR sits below the 25th percentile of recent sessions.
2. Price forms a tight range for at least 30 minutes.
3. ATR turns up and price closes above range high.
4. Volume pace rises above the morning average.
5. Enter long on breakout or first retest.

### Short Entry
1. Morning ATR sits below the 25th percentile.
2. Price forms a tight range.
3. ATR turns up and price closes below range low.
4. Volume pace rises with the breakdown.
5. Enter short on breakdown or first retest.

## Exit Rules

- Target 1: 1x the compression range.
- Target 2: 2x the compression range or nearest daily level.
- Exit if ATR expansion fails and price returns into range.

## Filters

- Avoid immediately before known macro announcements.
- Best when broader market also expands.
- Skip if breakout occurs on one isolated print.

## Risk Management

Size using the range boundary as stop. Do not widen the stop after volatility expands.
