# 68 — Quality Growth Base

## Overview
Buy profitable growth companies forming constructive bases while fundamentals remain strong.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Revenue/earnings growth remains positive.
- Stock consolidates for 6-12 weeks above 200-day MA.
- 50-day MA flattens or turns up.
- Enter on base breakout with volume.
- Stop below base midpoint or breakout day low.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R.
- Hold runner while weekly trend remains intact.
- Exit if base fails on heavy volume.

## Filters
- Prefer improving margins and strong balance sheet.
- Avoid over-owned names during risk-off regimes.
- Confirm relative strength.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Quality improves selection but does not remove technical risk.
