# 66 — Ex-Dividend Gap Reclaim

## Overview
Buy when a stock gaps down on ex-dividend adjustment but quickly reclaims the adjusted support zone.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Ex-dividend gap is mechanical, not fundamental deterioration.
- Price holds adjusted support after the gap.
- Volume is normal or improving.
- Enter on reclaim of ex-dividend day high or short range high.
- Stop below ex-dividend low.

## Exit Rules
- Target pre-dividend adjusted resistance.
- Exit if ex-dividend low fails.
- Time stop after 2-3 weeks if no recovery.

## Filters
- Use adjusted charts.
- Avoid if dividend was accompanied by bad guidance.
- Prefer liquid, stable stocks.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-0.75%. Do not use unadjusted price levels for stops.
