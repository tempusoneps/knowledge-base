# 86 — Closing Auction Trap Reversal

## Overview

Trade the reversal that happens before ATC when traders chase a late breakout but the market fails to confirm into the closing auction. The edge is recognizing that late liquidity can trap impatient momentum entries.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 1-min or 5-min |
| Markets | VN30F, liquid HSX stocks |
| Context | Last 45 minutes before ATC |

### Short Entry
1. Price breaks HOD after 13:45.
2. Breakout volume is high but follow-through is weak.
3. Price falls back below the breakout level within 2-4 candles.
4. Enter short on failed retest from below.
5. Stop above late HOD.

### Long Entry
1. Price breaks LOD after 13:45.
2. Breakdown volume is high but continuation stalls.
3. Price reclaims the breakdown level.
4. Enter long on successful retest from above.
5. Stop below late LOD.

## Exit Rules

- Target VWAP, afternoon midpoint, or the prior range.
- Close before ATC unless the plan specifically includes auction risk.
- Exit if price re-breaks the late extreme.

## Filters

- Avoid when confirmed closing imbalance supports the breakout.
- Best on range days, not strong trend days.
- Require enough distance to target after fees.

## Risk Management

Use smaller size late in the day because time to recover from slippage is limited.
