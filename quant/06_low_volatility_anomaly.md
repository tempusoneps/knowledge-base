# 06 — Low Volatility Anomaly

## Overview
Hold lower-volatility stocks that deliver better risk-adjusted returns than high-volatility peers.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Rank stocks by trailing 3-6 month realized volatility.
- Long lowest-volatility bucket with positive trend.
- Rebalance monthly or quarterly.
- Optional short highest-volatility bucket.

## Exit Rules
- Exit when volatility rank rises above median or trend turns negative.
- Rebalance on schedule.

## Filters
- Exclude distressed low-vol stocks with liquidity problems.
- Sector cap to avoid utility/defensive overconcentration.

## Risk Management
Equal-risk weighting and maximum sector exposure.
