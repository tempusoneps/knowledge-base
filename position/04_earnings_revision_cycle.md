# 04 — Earnings Revision Cycle

## Overview
Hold stocks through a multi-quarter period of positive earnings revisions.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Consensus EPS or revenue estimates rise for at least two consecutive months.
- Price is above 50-day and 200-day MAs.
- Enter on weekly breakout or pullback to 10-week MA.
- Add after the next revision confirms the cycle.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if revisions turn negative for two reporting periods.
- Technical exit on weekly close below 30-week MA.

## Filters
- Prefer revisions backed by revenue growth, not cost cuts only.
- Avoid cyclical peaks where estimates are already stretched.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%-1.5%. Review position after every earnings update.
