# 35 — Anchored VWAP from Major Low

## Overview
Buy pullbacks to AVWAP anchored from a major market or stock low, using it as institutional cost basis.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Anchor VWAP from a major capitulation low.
- Price trends above AVWAP for several weeks.
- Pullback reaches AVWAP on declining volume.
- Enter on bullish reversal or reclaim above AVWAP.
- Stop below pullback low.

## Exit Rules
- Target prior high or 2R.
- Exit if price closes below AVWAP for 2 sessions.
- Trail with 20-day MA after rebound.

## Filters
- Stronger if market also holds its anchored VWAP.
- Avoid if AVWAP is flat and price chops through it.
- Prefer leaders in recovery.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. If AVWAP breaks, do not average down.
