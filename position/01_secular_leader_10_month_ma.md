# 01 — Secular Leader 10-Month MA Hold

## Overview
Hold long-term leaders while monthly price remains above a rising 10-month moving average.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock has 2-3 years of revenue/earnings leadership or clear industry dominance.
- Monthly close is above rising 10-month and 30-month MAs.
- Enter on pullback to the 10-month MA or breakout from a monthly base.
- Add only after a monthly higher high.

## Exit Rules
- Exit on monthly close below the 10-month MA plus relative strength breakdown.
- Take partial profits into vertical monthly extensions.

## Filters
- Prefer sectors with multi-year tailwinds.
- Avoid highly levered companies in tightening credit cycles.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%-2% of portfolio. Position trades need smaller size and wider stops.
