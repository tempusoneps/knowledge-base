# 92 — Earnings Shock Intraday Drift

## Overview

Trade continuation after an earnings surprise when the first pullback is shallow and volume confirms institutional repricing. The setup avoids chasing the first spike and waits for acceptance.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 5-min chart |
| Markets | Liquid stocks after earnings/news release |
| Context | Earnings beat/miss, guidance change, margin shock |

### Long Entry
1. Positive earnings shock creates a strong gap or opening drive.
2. Price holds above VWAP for at least 30 minutes.
3. First pullback retraces less than 50% of the impulse.
4. Volume on pullback is lower than volume on advance.
5. Enter long when price breaks the pullback high.

### Short / Sell Signal
1. Negative earnings shock creates a gap down or opening sell drive.
2. Price stays below VWAP for at least 30 minutes.
3. First bounce retraces less than 50%.
4. Volume on bounce is weak.
5. Enter short where possible or use as sell/avoid signal.

## Exit Rules

- Target measured move equal to first impulse.
- Exit if VWAP is reclaimed/lost against the trade.
- Take partials into prior daily support/resistance.

## Filters

- Confirm the earnings event is new information.
- Avoid if the move is already at daily limit with no tradable liquidity.
- Prefer surprises that change forward expectations, not one-off accounting gains.

## Risk Management

Use smaller size than normal because news repricing can be volatile.
