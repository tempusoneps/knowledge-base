# 53 — Options Gamma Pin Escape

## Overview
Trade a stock that has been pinned near a major options strike and then escapes the pin after expiry or catalyst.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock trades near a high open-interest strike for several sessions.
- Expiry passes or catalyst breaks the pin.
- Price closes outside the pinned range with volume.
- Enter in breakout direction on close or first retest.
- Stop inside the pinned range.

## Exit Rules
- Target 1x pinned range height.
- Exit if price returns into the range.
- Take partial before next major strike.

## Filters
- Requires reliable options open-interest data.
- Best in liquid optionable stocks.
- Avoid if breakout is only one low-volume print.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-0.75%. Options positioning changes quickly.
