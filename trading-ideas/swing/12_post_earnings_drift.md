# 12 — Post-Earnings Drift

## Overview
Trade the tendency for stocks with meaningful earnings surprises to drift in the surprise direction for days or weeks.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Confirm a genuine earnings surprise.
- Price reaction is positive for long, negative for short/sell signal.
- Wait for 2-5 day consolidation after the event.
- Enter in the direction of the surprise when consolidation breaks.
- Stop beyond consolidation opposite side.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R or next weekly level.
- Exit if price retraces the full earnings reaction.
- Use time stop after 15-20 trading days if no progress.

## Filters
- Stronger when analysts revise forecasts after earnings.
- Avoid if market trend strongly opposes the trade.
- Prefer liquid stocks.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-1%. Do not enter before the report.
