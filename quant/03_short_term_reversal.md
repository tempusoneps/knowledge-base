# 03 — Short-Term Reversal

## Overview
Buy short-term losers and sell short-term winners inside a liquid universe when mean reversion dominates.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Rank 5-day return among liquid stocks.
- Long worst performers with no negative news filter.
- Short or avoid strongest short-term performers.
- Hold 2-5 trading days.

## Exit Rules
- Exit on mean reversion to universe median.
- Stop if loss exceeds 1.5x average 5-day volatility.

## Filters
- Avoid earnings/news gap names.
- Exclude limit-up/limit-down or illiquid stocks.

## Risk Management
Use small positions and broad diversification.
