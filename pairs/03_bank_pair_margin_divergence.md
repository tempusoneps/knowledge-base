# 03 — Bank Pair Margin Divergence

## Overview
Pair trade banks when net interest margin or asset-quality trends diverge.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Compare two banks with similar size or market exposure.
- Long bank with improving NIM/NPL trend.
- Short bank with deteriorating NIM/NPL trend.
- Enter when price ratio breaks a multi-week range.

## Exit Rules
- Exit after earnings if divergence closes.
- Stop if ratio returns inside range.

## Filters
- Avoid mismatched state/private policy exposure unless intentional.
- Watch rate-cycle sensitivity.

## Risk Management
Use beta-adjusted sizing and cap financial-sector concentration.
