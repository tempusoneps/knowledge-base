# 13 — Earnings Miss Relief Reversal

## Overview
Buy a stock after bad earnings if the market expected worse and price reverses higher, creating a short-covering and re-rating setup.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Earnings headline is weak or mixed.
- Stock gaps down or opens weak but closes strong.
- Volume is above average and price reclaims VWAP/previous close on daily basis.
- Enter above the reversal day high.
- Stop below reversal day low.

## Exit Rules
- Target prior resistance or 2R.
- Exit if price closes below reversal low.
- Trail after reclaiming the 50-day MA.

## Filters
- Best when valuation was already depressed.
- Avoid if guidance deteriorates materially.
- Require broad market stability.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%. This is a contrarian setup; keep invalidation strict.
