# 08 — Quality Compounder Pullback

## Overview
Buy high-quality compounders during broad market corrections when long-term fundamentals remain intact.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Company has high ROE/ROIC, stable margins, and low leverage.
- Price pulls back to 30-week or 10-month MA.
- Relative strength holds above prior major low.
- Enter on weekly reversal from support.

## Exit Rules
- Exit only on fundamental deterioration or monthly trend break.
- Trim if valuation reaches extreme percentile.

## Filters
- Avoid quality traps with slowing growth.
- Prefer recurring revenue or durable demand.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%-2%. Use staggered entries instead of all-in buying.
