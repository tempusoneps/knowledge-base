# 18 — Liquidity Growth Equity Beta

## Overview
Increase equity beta when money/liquidity growth improves and price confirms.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Money supply, credit growth, or liquidity proxy turns up.
- Equity index reclaims 200-day MA.
- Breadth improves.
- Enter index or high-quality cyclicals on pullback.

## Exit Rules
- Reduce exposure if liquidity proxy rolls over.
- Stop if index loses 200-day MA.

## Filters
- Avoid if inflation forces policy tightening.
- Confirm with credit spreads.

## Risk Management
Scale exposure gradually; liquidity data can be lagged or revised.
