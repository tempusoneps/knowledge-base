# 115 — No-Pullback Trend Entry

## Overview

Strong trend days sometimes never offer a normal pullback. This strategy enters after controlled sideways pauses instead of waiting for a deeper retracement that may not come.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 1-min execution, 5-min context |
| Markets | VN30F, liquid stocks |
| Context | One-directional trend day |

### Long Entry
1. Price is above VWAP and above rising 9-EMA.
2. Market has made at least two higher highs without deep pullback.
3. Price pauses sideways for 5-15 minutes.
4. Enter long when the pause high breaks.
5. Stop below the pause low.

### Short Entry
1. Price is below VWAP and below falling 9-EMA.
2. Market has made at least two lower lows.
3. Price pauses sideways for 5-15 minutes.
4. Enter short when the pause low breaks.
5. Stop above the pause high.

## Exit Rules

- Target 1x pause height first, then trail.
- Exit if price breaks the opposite side of the pause.
- Take partials into emotional extension candles.

## Filters

- Avoid if pause forms after a climactic volume spike.
- Best before 11:00 or after 13:30.
- Require broad market confirmation.

## Risk Management

Use small size because entries are not discounted. The tight pause stop is mandatory.
