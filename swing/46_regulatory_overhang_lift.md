# 46 — Regulatory Overhang Lift

## Overview
Buy when a long-standing regulatory risk is resolved and price breaks out of a depressed base.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock or sector has been held down by known regulatory overhang.
- Official resolution or clarity arrives.
- Price gaps or closes above base resistance with volume.
- Enter on first pullback that holds above breakout level.
- Stop below pullback low.

## Exit Rules
- Target upper end of old valuation range or 3R.
- Exit if price loses breakout base.
- Use time stop if no follow-through within 4 weeks.

## Filters
- Confirm overhang is actually resolved, not delayed.
- Avoid if fundamentals deteriorated during the overhang.
- Prefer sector-wide repricing.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Do not chase huge first-day moves without pullback.
