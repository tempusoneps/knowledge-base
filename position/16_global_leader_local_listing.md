# 16 — Global Leader Local Listing

## Overview
Own local-listed companies that are global or regional category leaders and continue compounding through cycles.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Company has durable market share and export/regional reach.
- Long-term ROIC remains above peers.
- Monthly chart forms higher highs and higher lows.
- Enter on 10-month MA pullback or multi-month breakout.

## Exit Rules
- Exit on structural market share loss.
- Technical exit on monthly trend break.

## Filters
- Avoid leaders with governance discount that keeps widening.
- Prefer liquidity suitable for position size.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%-2%. Review thesis quarterly, not daily.
