# 77 — Relative Strength Line Pullback Hold

## Overview
Buy when price pulls back but the relative strength line holds firm, showing the stock is quietly outperforming.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock is in an uptrend above 50-day MA.
- Price pulls back 5%-12%.
- RS line stays flat or makes higher low.
- Enter when price breaks pullback trendline.
- Stop below pullback low.

## Exit Rules
- Target prior high first.
- Exit if RS line breaks its higher low.
- Trail with 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Prefer market pullbacks where leaders hold up.
- Avoid stock-specific bad news.
- Confirm sector remains constructive.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. RS strength supports selection, not stop placement.
