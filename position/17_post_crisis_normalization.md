# 17 — Post-Crisis Normalization

## Overview
Buy a sector or company after crisis conditions normalize and price exits a long bottoming range.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Crisis driver fades: credit stress, pandemic disruption, legal overhang, or demand shock.
- Fundamentals stabilize for at least two quarters.
- Price breaks above 30-week MA and base resistance.
- Enter on weekly breakout or first retest.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if crisis indicators re-emerge.
- Technical exit below base support.

## Filters
- Avoid firms permanently impaired by the crisis.
- Prefer survivors that gained share.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%. Recovery positions need proof, not just cheap valuation.
