# 74 — Liquidity Vacuum Breakout

## Overview

Trade a fast directional move when price breaks into an area with little resting liquidity and no recent volume. The absence of supply or demand can make the move travel farther than a normal breakout.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 5-min chart |
| Markets | VN30F, liquid stocks |
| Context | Break from balance into a low-volume zone |

### Long Entry
1. Price consolidates below a clear resistance shelf.
2. Above the shelf, prior intraday volume is thin.
3. Breakout candle closes above resistance with relative volume above 1.5x.
4. Enter on close or first retest of the shelf.
5. Stop inside the old range.

### Short Entry
1. Price consolidates above a clear support shelf.
2. Below the shelf, prior intraday volume is thin.
3. Breakdown candle closes below support with strong volume.
4. Enter on close or first retest from below.
5. Stop inside the old range.

## Exit Rules

- First target: next visible high-volume node or prior day level.
- Trail with 9-EMA on 5-min during fast moves.
- Exit if price returns into the old range for 2 candles.

## Filters

- Best when index direction confirms.
- Avoid if the low-volume area is caused only by bad data or halted trading.
- Skip if spread widens after breakout.

## Risk Management

Use fixed 1R partials because vacuum moves can reverse sharply once liquidity returns.
