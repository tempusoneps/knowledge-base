# 03 — 50-Day Line Reclaim

## Overview
Buy a stock that temporarily loses the 50-day moving average but quickly reclaims it, trapping weak sellers.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Prior trend is positive and stock has not broken major weekly structure.
- Price closes below the 50-day MA for 1-5 sessions.
- Selling dries up below the line.
- Enter when price closes back above the 50-day MA with improved volume.
- Stop below the reclaim low.

## Exit Rules
- Target prior swing high first.
- Exit if price closes back below the 50-day MA within 3 sessions.
- Trail with 20-day MA after target 1.

## Filters
- Stronger if reclaim aligns with sector strength.
- Avoid if earnings gap caused the breakdown.
- Skip if 50-day MA is falling steeply.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-1%. Reclaims should work quickly; do not give them excessive time.
