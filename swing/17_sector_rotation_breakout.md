# 17 — Sector Rotation Breakout

## Overview
Buy stocks in a sector that begins outperforming after a long period of neglect, as capital rotates into a new theme.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Sector relative strength vs market breaks a 3-6 month downtrend.
- Multiple sector names reclaim 50-day or 200-day MA.
- Candidate forms a clean base.
- Enter on candidate breakout with sector ETF/index confirmation.
- Stop below base low or breakout level.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R or next weekly resistance.
- Exit if sector relative strength fails back below breakout.
- Trail while sector breadth improves.

## Filters
- Avoid one-stock sector moves.
- Prefer sectors with catalyst: policy, commodity, rates, earnings cycle.
- Confirm liquidity.

## Risk Management
Limit exposure to one rotation theme. Correlation risk is high.
