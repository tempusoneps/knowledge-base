# 05 — Producer vs Consumer Commodity Pair

## Overview
Long commodity producers and short commodity consumers when input/output price trends create earnings divergence.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Commodity breaks a 3-month trend.
- Producer margins should improve; consumer margins should compress.
- Ratio of producer/consumer breaks above resistance.
- Enter beta-adjusted long/short.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if commodity trend reverses.
- Close when spread reaches historical extreme.

## Filters
- Confirm producer is not heavily hedged.
- Avoid consumer with pricing power that offsets cost pressure.

## Risk Management
Commodity-linked pairs can gap; keep position size conservative.
