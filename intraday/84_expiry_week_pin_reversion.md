# 84 — Expiry-Week Pin Reversion

## Overview

During derivatives expiry week, VN30F can gravitate toward high open-interest or psychologically important settlement zones. This setup trades intraday overextensions away from a likely pin area only after momentum weakens.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 5-min chart |
| Markets | VN30F |
| Context | Expiry week, especially final two sessions |

### Long Entry
1. Identify a likely pin zone from round number, prior settlement, or heavy open interest.
2. Price moves sharply below the zone in the morning.
3. Selling pressure fails to create new lows after 2-3 attempts.
4. Enter long on reclaim of a 5-min resistance level.
5. Stop below the failed low.

### Short Entry
1. Identify a likely pin zone.
2. Price moves sharply above the zone.
3. Buying pressure fails to create new highs after 2-3 attempts.
4. Enter short on breakdown from local support.
5. Stop above the failed high.

## Exit Rules

- Primary target: pin zone.
- Take partials before the zone if price slows.
- Exit all before ATC if settlement mechanics become unpredictable.

## Filters

- Do not force the trade on non-expiry weeks.
- Avoid if a major macro/news event overrides expiry behavior.
- Require clear exhaustion away from the pin.

## Risk Management

Risk 0.5%-0.75%. Treat pinning as a tendency, not a certainty.
