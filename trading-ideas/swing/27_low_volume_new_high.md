# 27 — Low-Volume New High Warning Fade

## Overview
Use weak-volume new highs as a sell or short-watch signal when price extends without sponsorship.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock makes a new 3-month or 52-week high.
- Breakout volume is below 20-day average.
- Price closes back below breakout level within 1-3 sessions.
- Enter short where available or exit longs on failed retest.
- Stop above failed breakout high.

## Exit Rules
- Target 20-day MA first.
- Second target: base midpoint.
- Exit if price reclaims the breakout level with volume.

## Filters
- Avoid fading strong bull-market leaders too early.
- Stronger if market breadth weakens.
- Confirm with bearish divergence or distribution days.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%. Failure must be visible; do not short just because volume is low.
