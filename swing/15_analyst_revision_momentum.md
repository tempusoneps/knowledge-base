# 15 — Analyst Revision Momentum

## Overview
Trade stocks receiving multiple positive estimate revisions, using price confirmation to avoid purely narrative trades.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- At least two positive revisions or target raises within a short period.
- Price holds above 50-day MA.
- Relative strength line trends higher.
- Enter on breakout above recent 2-4 week range.
- Stop below range low or 50-day MA.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R.
- Exit if price closes below 50-day MA.
- Time stop after 4 weeks without progress.

## Filters
- Stronger after earnings or new product/cycle catalyst.
- Avoid illiquid names with limited coverage.
- Confirm sector trend.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-1%. Revisions support the trade; price action triggers it.
