# 01 — Stage 2 Breakout

## Overview
Buy the transition from accumulation into an uptrend when price breaks above a multi-week base with volume.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Timeframe: daily chart, weekly trend filter.
- Price builds a base for at least 6 weeks above or near the 200-day MA.
- 50-day MA turns up and price closes above base resistance.
- Breakout volume is at least 1.5x the 20-day average.
- Enter on breakout close or first low-volume retest.

## Exit Rules
- Initial stop below breakout day low or base midpoint.
- Take partial profit at 2R-3R.
- Exit if price closes back inside the base for 2 days.

## Filters
- Prefer stocks outperforming VNINDEX over the last 3 months.
- Avoid late-stage bases after several prior breakouts.
- Skip if market index is below a falling 200-day MA.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-1% per position. Do not buy if stop distance exceeds 10%-12%.
