# 71 — 20-Day Breakout Trend Follow

## Overview
Buy a simple 20-day high breakout when market regime, volume, and relative strength support continuation.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock is above rising 50-day MA.
- Relative strength vs market is rising.
- Price closes at a 20-day high.
- Volume is above 20-day average or range expansion is clean.
- Enter on close or next-day pullback.

## Exit Rules
- Stop below 10-day low or breakout level.
- Trail with 20-day low for trend following.
- Exit if price closes below 50-day MA.

## Filters
- Avoid earnings within the next few sessions unless intended.
- Prefer sector leaders.
- Skip if breakout is far extended from support.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Use systematic exits to avoid emotional profit-taking.
