# 07 — Oil Shock Transport Short

## Overview
Short or underweight fuel-sensitive transport stocks when oil spikes and margins are at risk.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Oil rises above upper 3-month range.
- Transport sector relative strength breaks down.
- Enter short/underweight transport basket on support break.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if oil spike reverses.
- Cover if sector reclaims broken support.

## Filters
- Check fuel hedging and pass-through ability.
- Avoid if demand is accelerating enough to offset fuel costs.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-1%. Use baskets to reduce single-name event risk.
