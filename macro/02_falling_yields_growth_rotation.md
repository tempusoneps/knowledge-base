# 02 — Falling Yields Growth Rotation

## Overview
Buy growth or long-duration equities when yields trend lower and growth leadership confirms.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Long-term yields break below 50-day and 200-day MAs.
- Real yields or inflation expectations ease.
- Growth/value ratio turns up.
- Enter leading growth basket on breakout.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if yields reclaim breakdown level.
- Stop if growth/value ratio fails.

## Filters
- Avoid unprofitable growth in credit stress.
- Prefer companies with earnings momentum.

## Risk Management
Use basket exposure or smaller single-name size due to rate sensitivity.
