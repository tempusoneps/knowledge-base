# 45 — Policy Catalyst Breakout

## Overview
Trade stocks that break out after a confirmed policy change creates a direct sector tailwind.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Policy/regulatory news is confirmed and relevant.
- Sector index or basket rallies with broad participation.
- Candidate breaks multi-week resistance.
- Enter on breakout close or first pullback hold.
- Stop below breakout level.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R or next weekly resistance.
- Exit if policy details weaken the original thesis.
- Trail while sector remains above 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Avoid vague policy rumors.
- Prefer direct beneficiaries, not distant second-order plays.
- Confirm liquidity and volume.

## Risk Management
Limit exposure to one policy theme; related names can reverse together.
