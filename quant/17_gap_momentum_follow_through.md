# 17 — Gap Momentum Follow-Through

## Overview
Follow large gaps that historically continue rather than fade based on volume and catalyst filters.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Gap exceeds 2x average gap.
- Volume in first hour exceeds threshold.
- Price holds above gap midpoint for long setup.
- Enter on break of opening range in gap direction.

## Exit Rules
- Exit after 3-10 days or on loss of gap midpoint.
- Stop below opening range for longs.

## Filters
- Separate earnings gaps from non-news gaps.
- Require sufficient liquidity.

## Risk Management
Use smaller size due to gap volatility.
