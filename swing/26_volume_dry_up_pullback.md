# 26 — Volume Dry-Up Pullback

## Overview
Buy an orderly pullback in an uptrend when volume dries up, showing sellers have lost pressure.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock is above rising 50-day MA.
- Pullback lasts 3-10 sessions.
- Volume falls below 20-day average near support.
- Enter on break of pullback trendline or bullish reversal day.
- Stop below pullback low.

## Exit Rules
- Target prior high first.
- Exit if pullback low fails.
- Trail using 20-day MA if breakout follows.

## Filters
- Best in leading stocks.
- Avoid if pullback volume expands.
- Confirm market is not in broad distribution.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Low volume alone is not entry; require upside trigger.
