# 15 — Policy Reform Re-Rating

## Overview
Hold beneficiaries of structural policy reform after confirmation in both fundamentals and price.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Reform is official and changes earnings outlook.
- Sector breadth improves for several weeks.
- Candidate breaks a 6-12 month base.
- Enter on weekly close above resistance.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if implementation stalls or price loses base.
- Trim after policy optimism becomes crowded.

## Filters
- Prefer direct beneficiaries.
- Avoid companies where valuation already prices full reform.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%. Policy trades carry headline gap risk.
