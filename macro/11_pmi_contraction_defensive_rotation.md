# 11 — PMI Contraction Defensive Rotation

## Overview
Rotate toward defensives when activity data deteriorates and cyclical leadership breaks.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- PMI falls for 2-3 readings or drops below 50.
- Defensive/cyclical ratio breaks above 3-month range.
- Enter defensive ETF/basket or reduce cyclical exposure.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if PMI stabilizes and cyclicals reclaim leadership.
- Stop if defensive ratio fails breakout.

## Filters
- Avoid if policy stimulus creates immediate cyclical reversal.
- Confirm with earnings revisions.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1.25%. Defensive trades can lag during liquidity rallies.
