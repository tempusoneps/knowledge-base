# 17 — Dividend Yield Spread Pair

## Overview
Trade relative value between stable dividend stocks when yield spread reaches an unusual extreme.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Select similar business-risk dividend stocks.
- Compute dividend yield spread versus history.
- Long higher-yield undervalued stock; short lower-yield expensive peer where feasible.
- Enter when price ratio starts reverting.

## Exit Rules
- Exit when yield spread returns to median.
- Stop if dividend sustainability changes.

## Filters
- Avoid dividend traps.
- Adjust for ex-dividend dates and payout policy.

## Risk Management
Keep beta and sector exposure balanced. Dividends can distort short economics.
