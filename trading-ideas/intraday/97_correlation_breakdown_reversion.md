# 97 — Correlation Breakdown Reversion

## Overview

Trade a temporary dislocation between two historically correlated instruments when one moves sharply and the other does not confirm. The setup waits for the stretched instrument to lose momentum before betting on convergence.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 5-min chart |
| Markets | Correlated sector pairs or VN30F vs cash proxy |
| Metric | Intraday spread or ratio |

### Long Spread Reversion
1. Pair normally moves together intraday.
2. Instrument A drops sharply while Instrument B stays stable.
3. A stops making new lows and reclaims a micro resistance.
4. Enter long A, or pair long A/short B where practical.
5. Stop below A's dislocation low.

### Short Spread Reversion
1. Instrument A spikes while B does not confirm.
2. A fails to make a second high.
3. Enter short A, or pair short A/long B where practical.
4. Stop above A's dislocation high.

## Exit Rules

- Target spread returning to intraday mean.
- Exit if the lagging instrument begins confirming the dislocation.
- Use time stop after 30 minutes.

## Filters

- Require historically stable relationship.
- Avoid single-name news that justifies the divergence.
- Use liquid instruments only.

## Risk Management

Spread trades still carry directional risk. Cap size and avoid overnight exposure.
