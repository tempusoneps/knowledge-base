# 73 — ATR Trailing Trend Swing

## Overview
Enter a confirmed trend and use ATR trailing stops to stay with the move without guessing targets.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock is above rising 50-day and 200-day MAs.
- Price breaks a recent swing high.
- ATR is stable or expanding moderately.
- Enter on breakout or first pullback.
- Initial stop 2 ATR below entry or below swing low.

## Exit Rules
- Trail stop 2-3 ATR below highest close for longs.
- Exit on stop or major reversal candle.
- Optional partial at 3R.

## Filters
- Avoid stocks with frequent gap risk.
- Best for liquid trend leaders.
- Do not use too tight ATR in volatile names.

## Risk Management
Position size from ATR stop. Accept giving back some open profit to catch larger trends.
