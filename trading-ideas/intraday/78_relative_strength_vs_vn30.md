# 78 — Relative Strength vs VN30 Trend Follow

## Overview

Buy stocks that hold firm while VN30 pulls back, or short/avoid stocks that cannot bounce while VN30 rallies. The edge comes from selecting the strongest instrument before the index resumes direction.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 5-min chart |
| Markets | VN30 components and liquid HSX stocks |
| Benchmark | VN30 or VNINDEX |

### Long Entry
1. VN30 pulls back toward VWAP or a 5-min support.
2. The stock stays above its own VWAP and does not break the prior swing low.
3. Relative strength line versus VN30 makes a new intraday high.
4. Enter when VN30 stabilizes and the stock breaks its micro range high.
5. Stop below the stock's relative-strength pullback low.

### Short Entry
1. VN30 bounces toward resistance.
2. The stock remains below VWAP and cannot reclaim its prior swing high.
3. Relative strength line makes a new intraday low.
4. Enter when VN30 stalls and the stock breaks its micro range low.
5. Stop above the weak bounce high.

## Exit Rules

- Target the stock's HOD/LOD or a measured move from the micro range.
- Exit if relative strength reverses for two 5-min candles.

## Filters

- Best when the stock has clean liquidity and low spread.
- Avoid if the stock is halted or affected by separate news.
- Prefer alignment with sector direction.

## Risk Management

Use the benchmark only as confirmation; stops must be based on the traded symbol.
