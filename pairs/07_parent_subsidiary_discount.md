# 07 — Parent-Subsidiary Discount Pair

## Overview
Trade dislocation between a listed parent and listed subsidiary when the implied holding-company discount stretches.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Estimate parent's look-through value from subsidiary stake.
- Compare discount to historical range.
- Long parent/short subsidiary when discount is too wide.
- Short parent/long subsidiary when discount is unusually narrow.

## Exit Rules
- Exit when discount returns to median.
- Stop if ownership, tax, or governance thesis changes.

## Filters
- Adjust for parent debt and non-listed assets.
- Avoid illiquid subsidiary shares.

## Risk Management
Use conservative sizing because holding-company discounts can persist.
