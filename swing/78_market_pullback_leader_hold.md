# 78 — Market Pullback Leader Hold

## Overview
Buy leaders that refuse to break down during a market pullback, then trigger higher as the index stabilizes.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Market index pulls back to 20-day or 50-day MA.
- Candidate holds above its own 20-day or 50-day MA.
- Relative strength makes a new high during market weakness.
- Enter when market stabilizes and candidate breaks short-term high.
- Stop below candidate pullback low.

## Exit Rules
- Target prior high or 2R-3R.
- Exit if market resumes breakdown and candidate loses support.
- Trail with 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Prefer top-ranked sector leaders.
- Avoid defensive holds if seeking growth momentum.
- Confirm liquidity.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Start with partial size if market is not fully recovered.
