# 80 — Sector Breadth Thrust

## Overview

Trade sector-wide momentum when a large majority of stocks in one sector turn positive at the same time. A single stock breakout is easier to trust when the whole sector is participating.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 5-min chart |
| Markets | Banking, securities, real estate, steel, oil and gas |
| Breadth Metric | Percent of sector stocks above VWAP or above open |

### Long Entry
1. At least 70% of sector stocks trade above VWAP.
2. Sector leader breaks HOD or holds a strong opening drive.
3. Candidate stock pulls back to VWAP or a 9-EMA while breadth remains strong.
4. Enter when the stock breaks the pullback high.
5. Stop below VWAP or the pullback low.

### Short Entry
1. At least 70% of sector stocks trade below VWAP.
2. Sector leader breaks LOD or rejects VWAP.
3. Candidate stock bounces weakly into VWAP or 9-EMA.
4. Enter on breakdown from the bounce.
5. Stop above VWAP or the bounce high.

## Exit Rules

- Target previous day high/low, HOD/LOD, or 2R.
- Exit if sector breadth drops below 55%.
- Trail using 5-min higher lows/lower highs.

## Filters

- Best during broad market risk-on/risk-off sessions.
- Avoid if only one mega-cap is moving the sector index.
- Require tradable liquidity in the candidate.

## Risk Management

Limit exposure to one sector theme at a time to avoid hidden concentration risk.
