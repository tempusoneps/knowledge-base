# 62 — Pair Momentum Divergence

## Overview
Buy the stronger stock in a correlated pair when it breaks out while the weaker one fails to confirm.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Pair belongs to same sector or business model.
- Strong stock makes a new 3-month high.
- Weak stock remains below resistance or 50-day MA.
- Enter strong stock on pullback or breakout continuation.
- Stop below strong stock's breakout level.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R.
- Exit if weak stock catches up and leadership disappears.
- Trail with 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Stronger when fundamentals support the leader.
- Avoid crowded pairs during broad sector panic.
- Confirm sector trend is positive.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Do not buy the weak stock just because it is cheaper.
