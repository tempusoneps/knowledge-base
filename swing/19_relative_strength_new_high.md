# 19 — Relative Strength New High

## Overview
Buy stocks whose relative strength line makes a new high before price does, signaling hidden leadership.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock is near a multi-week resistance level.
- Relative strength vs market breaks to a new 3-6 month high.
- Price forms a tight handle or short base.
- Enter on price breakout after RS confirmation.
- Stop below handle or base low.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R.
- Exit if RS line loses breakout.
- Trail with 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Prefer broad market uptrend or early recovery.
- Avoid if absolute price trend is still below falling 200-day MA.
- Confirm volume on price breakout.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. RS is a filter; price still defines entry and stop.
