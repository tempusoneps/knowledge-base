# 19 — Carry Unwind Risk-Off

## Overview
Trade risk-off when high-yielding FX or crowded carry assets start unwinding.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Carry basket breaks below 50-day MA.
- Funding currency strengthens.
- Equity volatility rises.
- Enter defensive FX/asset hedge or reduce risky exposure.

## Exit Rules
- Exit when carry basket reclaims 50-day MA.
- Cover hedges if volatility normalizes.

## Filters
- Confirm move is not only a one-day data shock.
- Watch central-bank communication.

## Risk Management
Carry unwinds move fast; use liquid instruments and predefined stop.
