# 48 — Weekly S/R Intraday Retest

## Overview

Intraday price action can often seem chaotic, but it frequently adheres strictly to higher-timeframe levels. A **Weekly Support/Resistance Level** is established on the weekly chart and represents major institutional boundaries. When intraday price action approaches one of these macro levels for the first time in a week, it almost always triggers a significant reaction (a bounce or a rejection), offering a high-probability intraday mean-reversion trade.

---

## Concept

```
The Logic:
  Intraday traders focus on the 5-min chart.
  Swing traders focus on the Daily chart.
  Institutions often focus on the Weekly/Monthly charts.
  When price hits a Weekly level, the institutional algorithms trigger, overpowering the intraday noise.

Levels to Watch (Pre-Market Prep):
  1. Previous Week's High (PWH)
  2. Previous Week's Low (PWL)
  3. Major Weekly Swing Highs/Lows from the past 1-3 months.
```

**Trade Logic:** Fade the intraday trend when it slams into a massive Weekly wall, anticipating that the macro level will hold on the first test.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value                                          |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Timeframe | Weekly (for levels), 5-min (for entry)         |
| Markets   | VN30F, Major VN30 Constituents                 |
| Context   | First test of the level in the current week    |

### Long Entry (Weekly Support Bounce)
1. **Prep:** Draw a line at the Previous Week's Low (PWL) or a major weekly swing low.
2. **The Approach:** Intraday price drops aggressively toward this level.
3. **The Tell:** As price hits the weekly support, look for intraday exhaustion (RSI < 30).
4. **Trigger:** A bullish reversal candle (Pin bar, engulfing) forms directly on or slightly piercing the weekly level.
5. **Entry:** Go Long on the 5-min reversal candle close.
6. **Stop Loss:** 1 ATR (on the 5-min chart) below the weekly level.

### Short Entry (Weekly Resistance Rejection)
1. **Prep:** Draw line at Previous Week's High (PWH) or weekly resistance.
2. **The Approach:** Intraday price rallies hard into the level.
3. **The Tell:** RSI > 70.
4. **Trigger:** Bearish reversal candle forms at the level.
5. **Entry:** Go Short on the 5-min reversal candle close.
6. **Stop Loss:** 1 ATR above the weekly level.

---

## Exit Rules

- **Target 1:** The intraday VWAP or the midpoint of the day's range. Take 50%.
- **Target 2:** The opposing side of the intraday structure (e.g., if bouncing off the low, target the intraday high). Take 50%.
- **Time Stop:** Intraday only, close by 14:15.
- **Failure:** If a 15-min candle closes completely beyond the weekly level, the macro support has broken. Exit immediately.

---

## Filters

- [ ] **Crucial:** Only trade the **FIRST** touch of the weekly level for that specific week. Second and third touches are much more likely to break through.
- [ ] If the approach to the level is driven by a massive, unexpected macro news event (e.g., surprise interest rate change), do not blindly fade it.
- [ ] Check if the Daily trend aligns. If you are fading a Weekly Resistance, it works best if the Daily trend is already overextended or bearish.

## Edge & Statistics

- **Win rate:** 65-70% on the *first touch* only.
- **Edge:** You are using macro institutional boundaries to define your intraday risk. The market respects these levels heavily.

## Example Trade Log

```
Date:      2026-06-03 (Wednesday)
Symbol:    VN30F2607
Prep:      Previous Week's High (PWH) is at 1,295.
Context:   VN30F rallies strongly all morning, reaching 1,294.5 at 11:15.
Trigger:   Hits 1,295. Forms a massive shooting star on the 5-min chart. RSI hits 78.
Entry:     1,293 (Short, close of shooting star)
Stop:      1,298 (Above PWH)
Target:    1,282 (Daily VWAP)
Result:    Level holds perfectly. Price fades back to VWAP by 13:45. +WIN (+11 pts).
```

---

## Notes & Improvements
- Do your homework on weekends. Draw the PWH, PWL, and major weekly levels on all your primary tickers so you don't have to think about it during the week.
- A breakout *through* a Weekly level (that holds and retests) is a massive swing trading signal, but for intraday purposes, the first touch is almost always a fade.
