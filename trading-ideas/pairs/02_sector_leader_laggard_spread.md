# 02 — Sector Leader vs Laggard Spread

## Overview
Long the sector leader and short the laggard when leadership gap is widening with fundamental confirmation.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Pair belongs to same sector.
- Leader has stronger relative strength and earnings revisions.
- Ratio chart breaks a 3-month high.
- Long leader, short laggard using beta-adjusted notional.

## Exit Rules
- Exit when ratio loses 20-day MA.
- Take profit at 2R or prior ratio resistance.

## Filters
- Avoid if laggard has imminent catalyst.
- Prefer broad sector uptrend for cleaner long alpha.

## Risk Management
Keep sector net exposure controlled. Do not over-short hard-to-borrow laggards.
