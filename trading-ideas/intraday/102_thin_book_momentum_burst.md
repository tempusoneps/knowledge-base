# 102 — Thin Book Momentum Burst

## Overview

Trade a short-lived momentum burst when the order book becomes unusually thin in the direction of a confirmed move. This is a tactical setup for fast execution, not a slow swing idea.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 1-min |
| Markets | VN30F, top liquidity stocks |
| Context | Confirmed directional impulse |

### Long Entry
1. Price is above VWAP and breaks a micro resistance.
2. Ask-side depth above the market is unusually thin.
3. Prints show aggressive buying lifting offers.
4. Enter long immediately or on a one-candle pause.
5. Stop below the burst trigger candle.

### Short Entry
1. Price is below VWAP and breaks micro support.
2. Bid-side depth below market is unusually thin.
3. Prints show aggressive selling hitting bids.
4. Enter short immediately or on a one-candle bounce.
5. Stop above the trigger candle.

## Exit Rules

- Take profit quickly at 0.8R-1.5R or next liquidity cluster.
- Exit when the book refills against the move.
- Do not overstay after the burst slows.

## Filters

- Avoid if spread widens beyond normal.
- Best during opening drive or news-confirmed momentum.
- Skip if there is no clear catalyst or trend context.

## Risk Management

Use small size and hard stops. Thin books can produce both favorable movement and severe slippage.
