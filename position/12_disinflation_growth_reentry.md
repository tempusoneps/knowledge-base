# 12 — Disinflation Growth Re-Entry

## Overview
Buy long-duration growth assets when inflation and yields trend lower and price confirms.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Inflation surprises trend down.
- Long yields break below a 6-month moving average.
- Growth sector relative strength turns up.
- Enter leading growth stocks on monthly base breakout.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if yields reclaim uptrend and growth RS fails.
- Technical exit below 30-week MA.

## Filters
- Avoid unprofitable growth with poor funding access.
- Prefer firms with improving earnings revisions.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%. Macro regime trades require periodic review.
