# 113 — Breakout Retest with Absorption

## Overview

Trade a breakout only after the retest shows sellers or buyers being absorbed at the old level. This separates healthy retests from weak breakouts that simply drift back into the range.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 1-min and 5-min |
| Markets | VN30F, liquid stocks |
| Context | Fresh breakout from clear range |

### Long Entry
1. Price breaks above resistance with volume.
2. Pullback retests the old resistance from above.
3. Selling volume appears but price does not close back inside the range.
4. Enter long when a 1-min or 5-min candle breaks the retest high.
5. Stop below the retest low.

### Short Entry
1. Price breaks below support with volume.
2. Bounce retests old support from below.
3. Buying volume appears but price cannot reclaim the range.
4. Enter short when a candle breaks the retest low.
5. Stop above the retest high.

## Exit Rules

- Target measured move from the original range.
- Exit if retest level fails after entry.
- Trail once price clears the breakout extreme.

## Filters

- Best when retest happens within 15-45 minutes of breakout.
- Avoid retests with very low liquidity.
- Require market or sector alignment.

## Risk Management

Risk is defined by the retest, not the original range. Skip if the retest stop is too wide.
