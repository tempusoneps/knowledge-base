# 61 — Pairs Relative Value Reversion

## Overview
Trade mean reversion between two historically correlated stocks after the spread stretches and begins to normalize.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Pair has stable historical correlation and similar sector exposure.
- Price ratio or spread reaches extreme percentile.
- Stretched stock shows reversal candle or moving-average reclaim/failure.
- Enter long underperformer and short/avoid outperformer where practical.
- Stop if spread extends beyond extreme by predefined threshold.

## Exit Rules
- Target spread returning to 20-day or 50-day mean.
- Exit if fundamental news justifies divergence.
- Use time stop after 4-6 weeks.

## Filters
- Avoid pairs with different catalysts.
- Use liquid stocks only.
- Confirm borrow/short feasibility if pair trading.

## Risk Management
Size both legs by volatility. Spread trades can still lose directionally.
