# 06 — Capex Cycle Winner

## Overview
Hold companies positioned to benefit from a multi-year capital expenditure cycle.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Industry capex plans are rising for several quarters.
- Company has order backlog or pricing leverage.
- Stock breaks above a 6-12 month base.
- Enter on breakout or first 10-week MA pullback.

## Exit Rules
- Exit when backlog growth slows and price loses 30-week MA.
- Take partial profit after parabolic monthly extension.

## Filters
- Confirm balance sheet can support growth.
- Avoid firms with margin pressure despite revenue growth.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%. Monitor quarterly order intake and margin trend.
