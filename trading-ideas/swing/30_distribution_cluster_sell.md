# 30 — Distribution Cluster Sell

## Overview
Exit or short-watch a stock when repeated heavy-volume down days appear near highs, signaling institutional selling.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock is extended or near resistance.
- 3 or more distribution days occur within 3-5 weeks.
- Price fails to make progress despite high volume.
- Sell longs or enter short where available after support break.
- Stop for short above last lower high.

## Exit Rules
- Target 50-day MA or prior base top.
- Exit short if price reclaims broken support.
- Cover partial at 2R.

## Filters
- Stronger if market index also shows distribution.
- Avoid shorting during broad melt-up.
- Confirm no positive catalyst is pending.

## Risk Management
This is primarily a risk-reduction signal. Short size should be smaller than long swing size.
