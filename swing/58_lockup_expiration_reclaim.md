# 58 — Lockup Expiration Reclaim

## Overview
Buy after lockup-expiration selling pressure is absorbed and the stock reclaims its post-expiration range.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Known lockup expiration creates expected supply.
- Stock sells off or chops before/after expiration.
- Price stabilizes and forms a range.
- Enter on close above post-lockup range high.
- Stop below range low.

## Exit Rules
- Target prior weekly resistance.
- Exit if price falls back into range.
- Use time stop after 4-6 weeks.

## Filters
- Confirm actual lockup size and date.
- Avoid if insiders sell aggressively after expiration.
- Prefer improving fundamentals.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-0.75%. Supply events can take longer to clear than expected.
