# 114 — VWAP One-Touch Trend Day

## Overview

On strong trend days, price may touch VWAP only once before continuing. This setup treats the first VWAP touch as the highest-quality pullback and ignores later touches that often signal weakening trend.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 5-min chart |
| Markets | VN30F, liquid stocks |
| Context | Strong opening drive away from VWAP |

### Long Entry
1. Price opens strong and stays above VWAP for at least 45 minutes.
2. VWAP has a positive slope.
3. First touch or near-touch of VWAP rejects quickly.
4. Enter long when price breaks the rejection candle high.
5. Stop below VWAP touch low.

### Short Entry
1. Price opens weak and stays below VWAP.
2. VWAP has a negative slope.
3. First VWAP touch from below rejects.
4. Enter short when price breaks the rejection candle low.
5. Stop above VWAP touch high.

## Exit Rules

- Target retest of trend extreme.
- Target 2: measured extension if extreme breaks.
- Exit if price closes on the wrong side of VWAP.

## Filters

- Only trade the first clean VWAP touch.
- Avoid flat VWAP or choppy morning structure.
- Stronger with sector or breadth confirmation.

## Risk Management

Risk 0.75%. Do not add on second and third VWAP touches unless a new setup forms.
