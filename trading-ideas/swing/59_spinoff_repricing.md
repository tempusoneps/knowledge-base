# 59 — Spinoff Repricing

## Overview
Buy a spinoff after forced selling fades and the independent company starts to re-rate.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Spinoff begins trading and initial forced selling occurs.
- Price forms a 4-12 week base.
- Volume declines during base and relative strength turns up.
- Enter on breakout above base resistance.
- Stop below base midpoint or last higher low.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R or valuation re-rating level.
- Exit if base fails.
- Trail with 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Prefer clear standalone financials.
- Avoid highly levered spinoffs without cash flow.
- Watch insider ownership.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%. Spinoffs can be inefficient but data quality may be limited.
