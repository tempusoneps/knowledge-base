# 08 — Gold vs Real Yield Trade

## Overview
Trade gold or gold miners when real yields trend lower and gold confirms a breakout.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Real yields break below 50-day MA.
- USD is flat or weakening.
- Gold closes above 3-month resistance.
- Enter gold ETF/futures or miners with relative strength.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if real yields reclaim uptrend.
- Stop if gold falls back inside range.

## Filters
- Miners add equity-market risk; gold futures are cleaner macro exposure.
- Avoid crowded parabolic moves.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1.25%. Size miners smaller than gold exposure.
