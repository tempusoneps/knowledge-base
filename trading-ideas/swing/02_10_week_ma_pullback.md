# 02 — 10-Week Moving Average Pullback

## Overview
Buy a leading stock when it pulls back to its rising 10-week moving average during an established uptrend.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Weekly trend is up: price above rising 10-week and 30-week MAs.
- Pullback reaches the 10-week MA after a prior advance.
- Daily selling volume contracts near the average.
- Enter when price forms a daily higher high from the MA area.
- Stop below the pullback low or 10-week MA zone.

## Exit Rules
- First target: prior swing high.
- Trail remaining position below weekly higher lows.
- Exit if weekly close breaks the 10-week MA with heavy volume.

## Filters
- Best after first or second pullback in a new trend.
- Avoid if stock is below relative strength trendline.
- Avoid broad market correction phases.

## Risk Management
Keep risk under 1%. Position size from stop distance, not from conviction.
