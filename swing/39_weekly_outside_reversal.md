# 39 — Weekly Outside Reversal

## Overview
Trade a major sentiment shift when the weekly candle breaks both sides of the prior week and closes strongly in one direction.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Current week trades below prior low and above prior high, or vice versa.
- Bullish setup closes near weekly high after undercut.
- Bearish setup closes near weekly low after failed breakout.
- Enter next week on break of reversal week's high/low.
- Stop beyond reversal week opposite extreme.

## Exit Rules
- Target next weekly resistance/support.
- Take partial at 2R.
- Exit if price closes back inside reversal week midpoint.

## Filters
- Stronger at major support/resistance.
- Avoid if reversal week is excessively wide.
- Confirm with market regime.

## Risk Management
Use smaller size due to wide weekly stops.
