# 05 — Post-Earnings Drift Factor

## Overview
Rank stocks by standardized earnings surprise and hold in the direction of the surprise.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Compute earnings surprise versus consensus or prior trend.
- Long top surprise bucket; short/avoid bottom bucket.
- Enter after first post-earnings day to reduce gap noise.
- Hold 20-60 trading days.

## Exit Rules
- Exit at holding-period end or if price fully retraces reaction.
- Stop on severe adverse guidance update.

## Filters
- Require liquidity and clean earnings data.
- Sector-neutral ranking improves robustness.

## Risk Management
Limit single earnings-date cluster exposure.
