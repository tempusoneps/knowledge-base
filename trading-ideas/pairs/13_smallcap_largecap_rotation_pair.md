# 13 — Small Cap vs Large Cap Rotation Pair

## Overview
Trade rotation between small-cap and large-cap baskets based on liquidity and risk appetite.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Small/large ratio breaks a 3-month range.
- Breadth confirms inside the outperforming group.
- Long outperforming basket, short underperforming basket.
- Use volatility-adjusted exposure.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if ratio returns inside range.
- Take profit at prior ratio resistance/support.

## Filters
- Small caps need strong liquidity and credit conditions.
- Avoid periods with severe funding stress.

## Risk Management
Small-cap legs have higher slippage; haircut position size.
