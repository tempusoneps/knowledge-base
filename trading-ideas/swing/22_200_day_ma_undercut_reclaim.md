# 22 — 200-Day MA Undercut and Reclaim

## Overview
Buy a major moving-average shakeout when price briefly undercuts the 200-day MA and reclaims it with strength.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock has a long-term uptrend or broad base.
- Price undercuts the 200-day MA for 1-5 sessions.
- Reclaim day closes back above the 200-day MA.
- Volume improves on reclaim.
- Enter on reclaim close or next-day hold above the line.

## Exit Rules
- Target 50-day MA or prior swing high.
- Exit if price closes back below the 200-day MA.
- Trail after reclaiming 50-day MA.

## Filters
- Avoid stocks in clear downtrends with falling 200-day MA.
- Stronger when market index also reclaims support.
- Check for earnings risk.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-0.75%. Major support trades need strict invalidation.
