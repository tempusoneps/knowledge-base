# 79 — Failed Follow-Through Exit

## Overview
Use a failed follow-through day or failed breakout cluster as a market-level exit signal for swing exposure.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Market attempts a rally after correction.
- Follow-through day appears but leading stocks fail to break out.
- Index undercuts follow-through day low within 3-10 sessions.
- Reduce longs or enter defensive/short setups.
- Stop for shorts above failed rally high.

## Exit Rules
- Cover shorts or restore exposure if index reclaims failed level.
- Target prior index low or defensive rotation.
- Use time stop if market chops sideways.

## Filters
- Stronger when breadth deteriorates.
- Avoid reacting to one weak day without level break.
- Confirm with distribution days.

## Risk Management
This is primarily portfolio risk control. Reduce exposure before increasing short risk.
