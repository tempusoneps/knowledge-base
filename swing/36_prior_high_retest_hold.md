# 36 — Prior High Retest Hold

## Overview
Buy a breakout retest when a prior major high flips into support.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock breaks above a multi-month high.
- Pullback retests the old high within 1-4 weeks.
- Retest volume is lower than breakout volume.
- Enter on bullish daily reversal from the old high.
- Stop below retest low.

## Exit Rules
- Target retest of breakout high first.
- Second target: measured move from base.
- Exit if old high fails on close.

## Filters
- Stronger when old high aligns with 20-day or 50-day MA.
- Avoid if breakout was low-volume.
- Confirm sector trend.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Do not buy if retest slices through support without reversal.
