# 41 — Rate-Sensitive Rotation

## Overview
Trade sectors that benefit from a change in interest-rate expectations, using price confirmation instead of macro opinion alone.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Rates or bond yields break a multi-week trend.
- Rate-sensitive sector starts outperforming the market.
- Candidate stock reclaims 50-day MA or breaks a base.
- Enter on daily close above resistance.
- Stop below breakout level or 50-day MA.

## Exit Rules
- Target next weekly resistance or 2R-3R.
- Exit if yield trend reverses against the thesis.
- Trail with 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Match sector logic: banks, real estate, utilities, growth stocks may react differently.
- Avoid single-stock news conflicts.
- Confirm sector breadth.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Macro-sensitive trades can gap; avoid oversizing.
