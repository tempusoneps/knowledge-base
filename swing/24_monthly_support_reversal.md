# 24 — Monthly Support Reversal

## Overview
Trade a swing reversal from a major monthly support zone after daily price action confirms demand.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Identify monthly support from multi-year chart.
- Price approaches the zone after a prolonged decline.
- Daily chart forms bullish reversal, higher low, or reclaim candle.
- Enter above daily reversal high.
- Stop below monthly support rejection low.

## Exit Rules
- Target first weekly resistance.
- Exit if monthly support breaks on weekly close.
- Take partial at 2R.

## Filters
- Avoid value traps with deteriorating fundamentals.
- Stronger if sector stops underperforming.
- Use adjusted charts for dividends/splits.

## Risk Management
Monthly levels can require wide stops; size down accordingly.
