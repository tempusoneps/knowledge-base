# 18 — Market Cap Upgrade Path

## Overview
Hold stocks likely to move from small/mid-cap status into larger institutional mandates.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Market cap and liquidity are rising steadily.
- Fundamentals support institutional ownership.
- Stock forms a long base near highs.
- Enter on breakout with volume and relative strength.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if liquidity dries up or growth slows.
- Take partial after index inclusion or major fund crowding.

## Filters
- Avoid promotional small caps.
- Prefer clean governance and audited growth.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%. Liquidity can deteriorate fast in corrections.
