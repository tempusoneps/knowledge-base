# 08 — Volatility Contraction Pattern

## Overview
Trade a sequence of smaller pullbacks inside a base, signaling supply is drying up before a breakout.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock has a prior uptrend.
- Base shows 2-4 contractions, each shallower than the previous one.
- Volume declines during contractions.
- Enter on break above the final contraction high.
- Stop below final contraction low.

## Exit Rules
- Take partial at 2R.
- Exit if breakout fails below final contraction low.
- Trail with 20-day MA or weekly lows.

## Filters
- Prefer high relative strength stocks.
- Avoid names with heavy distribution in the base.
- Best when market volatility is falling.

## Risk Management
This setup works because risk is tight. Skip if final contraction stop is wider than 8%-10%.
