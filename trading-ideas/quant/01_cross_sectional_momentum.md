# 01 — Cross-Sectional Momentum

## Overview
Buy the strongest stocks and avoid or short the weakest stocks based on trailing relative returns.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Universe: liquid stocks only.
- Rank 6-month return excluding most recent 1 month.
- Long top decile/quintile; short or avoid bottom decile/quintile.
- Rebalance monthly.

## Exit Rules
- Exit when rank falls below median or at scheduled rebalance.
- Apply stop if position loses 2x expected monthly volatility.

## Filters
- Exclude suspended/illiquid stocks.
- Neutralize sector exposure if desired.

## Risk Management
Volatility weight positions and cap single-name exposure.
