# 80 — Portfolio Heat Throttle

## Overview
Scale swing exposure based on portfolio heat, correlation, and market regime instead of treating each setup independently.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Define maximum open risk across all positions.
- Add new longs only when existing positions are working and market regime is supportive.
- Reduce size when positions are highly correlated by sector/theme.
- Enter only A-grade setups when portfolio heat is near limit.
- Stop placement remains setup-specific.

## Exit Rules
- Cut weakest positions first when market breaks support.
- Reduce exposure if total open risk exceeds plan.
- Trail winners but avoid adding correlated risk late.

## Filters
- Track sector concentration.
- Avoid stacking many names with same catalyst.
- Reassess after major index distribution days.

## Risk Management
Keep total open risk commonly below 4%-6% of equity. This idea is a practical overlay for all swing systems.
