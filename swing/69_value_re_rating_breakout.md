# 69 — Value Re-Rating Breakout

## Overview
Buy undervalued stocks when price breaks out after a catalyst suggests the valuation discount may close.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock trades at discount to peers or history.
- Catalyst appears: earnings improvement, asset sale, policy change, buyback.
- Price breaks a multi-month base.
- Enter on breakout or first retest.
- Stop below base support.

## Exit Rules
- Target peer multiple range or 2R-3R.
- Exit if catalyst fails to improve fundamentals.
- Trail with 50-day MA.

## Filters
- Avoid cheap stocks with deteriorating cash flow.
- Prefer clear balance-sheet strength.
- Confirm institutional accumulation.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%. Cheap can stay cheap; wait for breakout.
