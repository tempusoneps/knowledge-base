# 91 — News Reopen Range Break

## Overview

After a stock pauses or becomes temporarily disorderly around a major announcement, the first few minutes after liquidity returns often define a tradable range. This setup trades acceptance outside that reopen range.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 1-min and 5-min |
| Markets | Liquid stocks with news catalyst |
| Context | Reopen after trading pause, sharp news spike, or disorderly auction |

### Long Entry
1. Wait for the first 5-10 minutes after liquidity normalizes.
2. Mark the reopen range high and low.
3. Price holds above VWAP or reclaims the range midpoint.
4. Enter long on a close above reopen range high.
5. Stop below the range midpoint or low.

### Short Entry
1. Mark the reopen range after news liquidity returns.
2. Price fails below VWAP or loses the range midpoint.
3. Enter short where available on close below reopen range low.
4. Stop above the range midpoint or high.

## Exit Rules

- Target 1x reopen range height.
- Exit if price returns into the range and holds for 2 candles.
- Use time stop if no follow-through within 20 minutes.

## Filters

- Avoid unclear or unverified rumors.
- Do not trade if spread remains abnormal.
- Prefer news with direct earnings, corporate action, or regulatory impact.

## Risk Management

Risk 0.5% or less. News trades carry gap and slippage risk even intraday.
