# 38 — Weekly Inside Bar Breakout

## Overview
Trade expansion after a weekly inside bar compresses below or above a major level.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Weekly candle is fully inside previous week's range.
- Inside bar forms after a trend or near a key breakout level.
- Enter long above inside-week high or short/sell below inside-week low.
- Confirm with daily close in the breakout direction.
- Stop on the opposite side of the inside week.

## Exit Rules
- Target 1x inside-week range.
- Exit if breakout reverses back inside the weekly range.
- Trail with weekly lows/highs.

## Filters
- Avoid very wide mother bars.
- Stronger with volume contraction during inside week.
- Trade in direction of higher-timeframe trend when possible.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-1%. Weekly ranges can be wide; size accordingly.
