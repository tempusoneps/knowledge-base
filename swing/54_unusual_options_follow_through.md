# 54 — Unusual Options Follow-Through

## Overview
Use unusual options activity as a watchlist filter, then enter only when stock price confirms with a breakout.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Unusual call activity for long, put activity for sell/short watch.
- Stock forms a tight range after the options print.
- Volume and price move in the same direction.
- Enter on break of range in options-flow direction.
- Stop on opposite side of range.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R or next weekly level.
- Exit if stock fails while options premium decays.
- Use time stop before option catalyst date if known.

## Filters
- Ignore activity that is clearly hedging.
- Prefer sweeps near ask plus stock accumulation.
- Confirm liquidity.

## Risk Management
Risk stock position normally; do not size based on options notional.
