# 18 — Liquidity-Adjusted Momentum

## Overview
Rank momentum by return adjusted for liquidity and transaction cost to avoid untradable winners.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Compute 6-month return.
- Penalize high spread, low turnover, and high impact cost.
- Buy top liquidity-adjusted momentum names.
- Rebalance monthly.

## Exit Rules
- Exit when adjusted rank falls below median.
- Stop if liquidity collapses.

## Filters
- Exclude suspended or limit-bound stocks.
- Sector caps reduce crowding.

## Risk Management
Position size scales with liquidity and volatility.
