# 76 — Multi-Day Squeeze to Trend

## Overview
Trade expansion after Bollinger Band width compresses for several weeks and price breaks with volume.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Bollinger Band width reaches 6-month low percentile.
- Price forms a tight range above 50-day MA for long.
- Enter long on close above range high; sell/short signal below range low.
- Volume expands on the break.
- Stop on opposite side of range.

## Exit Rules
- Target 1x-2x range height.
- Exit if price returns into range.
- Trail if expansion becomes trend.

## Filters
- Avoid compression directly before binary earnings.
- Stronger when market volatility also contracts.
- Confirm sector direction.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Compression trades need clean breakout and defined range.
