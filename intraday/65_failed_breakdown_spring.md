# 65 — The "Failed Breakdown" Reversal (Spring)

## Overview

In Wyckoff methodology, a **Spring** is a price action event where a stock drops below a defined support level (or trading range), triggers stop losses, but fails to continue downward, quickly reversing to close back inside the range. This "failed breakdown" traps bears and absorbs all available sell orders, creating an explosive vacuum of upside momentum.

---

## Concept

```
The Mechanics:
  1. A clear support level is established over hours or days.
  2. Price breaks below the support line.
  3. Bears (short sellers) jump in, expecting a massive dump.
  4. Institutional "Smart Money" absorbs the selling (buying the dip).
  5. The breakdown fails to follow through. Price snaps back above the support line.
  6. The trapped shorts panic cover, driving the price all the way to the top of the range.
```

**Trade Logic:** A failed move in one direction is the strongest indicator of a massive move in the opposite direction.

| Indicator      | Setting                      | Purpose                                  |

| Support Line   | Manual                       | Identify the key range boundary          |
| Candlesticks   | 5-min or 15-min              | Confirm the rejection (Spring)           |
| Volume         | 20-bar MA                    | Confirm absorption on the breakdown      |

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value                                          |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Timeframe | 15-min chart (best), 5-min (acceptable)        |
| Markets   | VN30F, Major HSX Stocks                        |
| Context   | Market must be in a trading range              |

### Long Entry (The Spring)
1. **Identify Support:** Price has bounced off a specific level at least twice.
2. **The Breakdown:** Price drops below the support level.
3. **The Observation (Crucial):** Watch how the price behaves below the line. It should NOT collapse. It should stall. Volume may be high, but price isn't moving down (absorption).
4. **The Trigger:** A 15-min candle closes firmly back **ABOVE** the support line.
5. **Entry:** Go Long on the close of the candle that reclaims the support.
6. **Stop Loss:** Just below the absolute low of the failed breakdown wick.

*(Note: The bearish equivalent of this is an Upthrust (Failed Breakout), which is covered similarly in Idea #63, but this focuses specifically on the Wyckoff Spring structure at major support).*

---

## Exit Rules

- **Target 1:** The midpoint of the trading range. Take 50%.
- **Target 2:** The Resistance level at the top of the trading range. Take remaining 50%.
- **Runner:** If the top of the range breaks, hold 10% for a macro trend change.
- **Failure:** If price reverses and breaks below the Spring's lowest wick, the thesis is completely invalidated. Exit immediately.

---

## Filters

- [ ] **Crucial:** The Spring must happen relatively quickly. If price breaks below support and stays there for 2 hours, it's not a Spring; it's a new downtrend. The snap-back should occur within 1 to 3 candles on a 15-min chart.
- [ ] If the breakdown occurs on low volume, and the snap-back also occurs on low volume, skip it. You want to see high volume on the Spring to confirm institutional absorption.

---

## Risk Management

| Rule             | Guideline                          |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Max Risk/Trade   | 1% of account equity               |
| Stop Placement   | Strict stop below the Spring low   |
| Min R:R          | 1:3 (Ranges offer massive targets) |

---

## Edge & Statistics

- **Win rate:** 60-65% in range-bound markets.
- **Edge:** By waiting for the breakdown to *fail*, you avoid being the liquidity for the institutions. The stop loss is exceptionally tight compared to the target (the top of the range).

## Example Trade Log

```
Date:      2026-06-18
Symbol:    VNM (HSX)
Context:   VNM has been ranging between 65,000 and 66,500 for two days.
The Break: At 10:15, price dumps to 64,600 (breaking 65k support). Volume spikes.
The Spring: Instead of continuing down, the next 15-min candle is a massive green engulfing bar, closing at 65,200 (reclaiming support).
Entry:     65,200 (Long)
Stop:      64,500 (Below the Spring low)
Target:    66,500 (Top of the range)
Result:    Trapped bears cover all day. Hits target at 14:15. +WIN (+1,300 pts).
```
