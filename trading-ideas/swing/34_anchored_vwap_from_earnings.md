# 34 — Anchored VWAP from Earnings

## Overview
Use AVWAP anchored to an earnings event as a swing support or resistance reference.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Anchor VWAP to a major earnings gap or reaction day.
- For longs, price stays above rising AVWAP and pulls back to it.
- Enter on bullish reversal from AVWAP.
- For shorts/sells, price rejects AVWAP from below.
- Stop beyond the AVWAP rejection candle.

## Exit Rules
- Target prior swing high/low.
- Exit if price accepts on the wrong side of AVWAP for 2 sessions.
- Trail after target 1.

## Filters
- Use only meaningful catalyst anchors.
- Stronger when AVWAP aligns with 50-day MA or support.
- Avoid noisy small-cap charts.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-1%. AVWAP is invalid when event thesis is invalid.
