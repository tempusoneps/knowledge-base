# 55 — Low Float Supply Squeeze

## Overview
Buy a low-float stock only after it forms a controlled base and breaks out with real volume.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Float is limited and daily liquidity is sufficient.
- Stock has a clear catalyst or theme.
- Base forms for at least 2-4 weeks.
- Enter on breakout above base high with volume expansion.
- Stop below breakout day low or base midpoint.

## Exit Rules
- Take partial at 2R.
- Trail aggressively with 10-day MA.
- Exit on high-volume reversal day.

## Filters
- Avoid pump-like illiquid names.
- Require clean filings and tradable spread.
- Stronger in speculative market regimes.

## Risk Management
Use half normal size. Low-float stocks gap and reverse violently.
