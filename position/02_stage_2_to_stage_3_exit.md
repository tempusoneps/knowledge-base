# 02 — Stage 2 to Stage 3 Exit

## Overview
Exit a long-term winner when the uptrend stops advancing and transitions into a topping range.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Existing position is in a mature Stage 2 uptrend.
- Price stops making monthly higher highs.
- 30-week MA flattens and distribution volume increases.
- Reduce when price breaks the lower edge of the topping range.

## Exit Rules
- Full exit on weekly close below 30-week MA.
- Re-enter only if price rebuilds a base and breaks out again.

## Filters
- Stronger signal if sector leadership also fades.
- Avoid overreacting to one normal pullback.

## Risk Management
Protect open profit. This is a portfolio-defense strategy, not a new short by default.
