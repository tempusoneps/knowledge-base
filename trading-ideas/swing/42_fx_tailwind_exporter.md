# 42 — FX Tailwind Exporter Swing

## Overview
Buy exporters or FX beneficiaries when currency movement improves expected margins and price confirms accumulation.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Relevant FX pair trends in favor of exporter margins.
- Sector or peer group starts outperforming.
- Stock forms a base above 50-day MA.
- Enter on breakout above base resistance.
- Stop below base midpoint or recent higher low.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R or weekly resistance.
- Exit if FX trend reverses materially.
- Trail with 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Confirm company has real FX exposure.
- Avoid firms with hedges that neutralize the tailwind.
- Prefer improving volume on breakout.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-1%. Macro tailwinds support only if price agrees.
