# 10 — Breadth Thrust System

## Overview
Use market breadth thrusts as systematic risk-on signals.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Compute percent of stocks above 20-day MA or advancer ratio.
- Buy index/leader basket when breadth moves from oversold to strong threshold within 10 days.
- Enter on close after signal.
- Hold while index remains above 20-day MA.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if breadth falls below neutral or index loses 50-day MA.
- Time stop after 6-8 weeks if no progress.

## Filters
- Require broad universe and clean data.
- Avoid signals during exchange-wide data distortions.

## Risk Management
Scale in if volatility remains elevated.
