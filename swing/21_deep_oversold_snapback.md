# 21 — Deep Oversold Snapback

## Overview
Buy a short-term swing rebound after a quality stock becomes deeply oversold but avoids structural breakdown.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock drops 15%-30% from recent high into weekly support.
- Daily RSI falls below 30, then turns up.
- Selling volume climaxes and next day holds above the low.
- Enter above the reversal day high.
- Stop below panic low.

## Exit Rules
- Target declining 10-day or 20-day MA.
- Exit if price breaks panic low.
- Take profits quickly; this is a rebound, not a long-term trend trade.

## Filters
- Avoid stocks with fresh fundamental damage.
- Prefer market index near support.
- Stronger when put/call or sentiment is washed out.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%. Oversold can become more oversold, so wait for price reversal.
