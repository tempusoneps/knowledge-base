# 18 — Laggard Catch-Up Swing

## Overview
Buy a laggard only after sector leaders have already broken out and the laggard confirms it is starting to participate.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Sector leaders are above 50-day MA and near highs.
- Laggard builds a base and stops making lower lows.
- Relative strength vs sector turns up.
- Enter on close above laggard's base resistance.
- Stop below base midpoint or last higher low.

## Exit Rules
- Target prior weekly resistance.
- Exit if leader stocks reverse sharply.
- Take profits faster than in true leaders.

## Filters
- Avoid structurally weak companies.
- Best in broad sector rallies.
- Do not buy merely because it is cheap.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-0.75%. Laggards deserve smaller size and quicker exits.
