# 15 — Month-End Rebalance Flow

## Overview
Systematically trade predictable month-end flows when equity/bond performance divergence implies rebalancing demand.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Estimate month-to-date equity vs bond performance.
- If equities outperform strongly, expect selling/underweight into month-end; reverse if underperform.
- Enter index trade in final 1-3 sessions when price confirms.
- Exit after rebalance window.

## Exit Rules
- Exit first session of new month or when flow target reached.
- Stop if price breaks against expected flow.

## Filters
- Works best after large cross-asset moves.
- Avoid if major macro event dominates.

## Risk Management
Use small size and strict time stop.
