# 16 — Earnings Pre-Announcement Drift

## Overview
Trade statistically persistent drift before earnings for stocks with strong prior momentum and revision trends.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Earnings date is known.
- Stock has positive 3-month momentum and upward estimate revisions.
- Enter 5-15 trading days before earnings.
- Exit before announcement unless explicitly taking event risk.

## Exit Rules
- Exit one day before earnings.
- Stop if price loses 20-day MA before event.

## Filters
- Avoid illiquid names and high gap-risk small caps.
- Require historical pre-earnings drift evidence.

## Risk Management
Never accidentally hold through earnings unless planned.
