# 13 — Policy Easing Risk-On

## Overview
Buy risk assets after credible policy easing when breadth and price confirm.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Central bank cuts rates, injects liquidity, or signals easing.
- Equity index reclaims 50-day MA.
- Breadth thrust confirms participation.
- Enter index/sector leaders on pullback or breakout.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if policy signal is reversed.
- Stop if index loses reclaim level.

## Filters
- Avoid easing caused by severe crisis unless credit stabilizes.
- Confirm currency impact.

## Risk Management
Scale in over several signals; do not assume first easing day is the bottom.
