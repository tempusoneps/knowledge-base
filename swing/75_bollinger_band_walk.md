# 75 — Bollinger Band Walk

## Overview
Buy strong trends that keep walking the upper Bollinger Band, entering on controlled pullbacks to the middle band.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Price repeatedly closes near upper Bollinger Band.
- Middle band slopes upward.
- Pullback touches middle band or 20-day MA.
- Enter on bullish reversal from middle band.
- Stop below pullback low.

## Exit Rules
- Target upper band retest.
- Exit if price closes below lower band or 50-day MA.
- Trail with 20-day MA.

## Filters
- Avoid mean-reversion regimes.
- Stronger with rising volume on advances.
- Use only liquid stocks.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%. Band walks can end abruptly; respect trend break.
