# 14 — Guidance Raise Follow-Through

## Overview
Buy stocks that raise forward guidance and show several days of institutional follow-through after the announcement.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Company raises revenue, margin, or profit guidance.
- Price closes above prior resistance on strong volume.
- Follow-through day occurs within 3-7 sessions.
- Enter on pullback to breakout level or break of follow-through high.
- Stop below pullback low or breakout level.

## Exit Rules
- Target next weekly resistance or 3R.
- Exit if stock loses the breakout level with volume.
- Hold runner while 20-day MA trends up.

## Filters
- Avoid guidance raises from one-off accounting effects.
- Prefer industry-wide tailwind.
- Confirm relative strength vs market.

## Risk Management
Risk 1% maximum. Do not chase if more than 10% above support.
