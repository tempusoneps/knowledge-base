# 12 — Quality vs Junk Spread

## Overview
Trade tightening or widening risk appetite via quality stocks versus low-quality/high-leverage stocks.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Risk-off: long quality basket, short junk basket.
- Risk-on: reverse only when breadth and credit improve.
- Enter on quality/junk ratio breakout.
- Size baskets by volatility.

## Exit Rules
- Exit when ratio returns to moving average or breaks trend.
- Stop on credit regime reversal.

## Filters
- Define quality using ROIC, leverage, profitability.
- Define junk systematically, not by opinion.

## Risk Management
Short junk baskets can squeeze. Cap gross and use basket diversification.
