# 14 — Commodity Cost Curve Winner

## Overview
Own low-cost producers during a commodity upcycle because they capture higher margins with lower survival risk.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Commodity price breaks a multi-year resistance.
- Company sits low on industry cost curve.
- Stock breaks above a long base with volume.
- Enter on breakout or first weekly pullback.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if commodity falls below its 10-month MA.
- Reduce when producer margins peak and price loses 30-week MA.

## Filters
- Avoid high-cost producers unless explicitly trading leverage.
- Check hedging and capex discipline.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%-1.5%. Commodity cycles are volatile; diversify across positions.
