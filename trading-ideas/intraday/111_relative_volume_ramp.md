# 111 — Relative Volume Ramp

## Overview

Trade early continuation when relative volume increases steadily before price fully breaks out. The ramp suggests participation is building, not appearing only after the move is obvious.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 5-min chart |
| Markets | Liquid HSX stocks |
| Metric | Current volume pace vs same time-of-day average |

### Long Entry
1. Price holds above VWAP and forms a tight range.
2. Relative volume rises for at least 3 consecutive 5-min bars.
3. Range highs are tested without heavy rejection.
4. Enter long on close above range high.
5. Stop below VWAP or range low.

### Short / Sell Entry
1. Price holds below VWAP in a tight range.
2. Relative volume rises while bounces weaken.
3. Range lows are tested repeatedly.
4. Enter short where possible, or sell/avoid, on close below range low.
5. Stop above VWAP or range high.

## Exit Rules

- Target 1x-2x range height.
- Exit if volume ramp collapses after breakout.
- Take partials at first daily level.

## Filters

- Avoid one-off block prints that distort volume pace.
- Stronger when sector volume also ramps.
- Skip if breakout candle is too extended from stop.

## Risk Management

Use the range as risk. Do not enter if the range is too wide for minimum 1:2 R:R.
