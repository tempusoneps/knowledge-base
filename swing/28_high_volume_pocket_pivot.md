# 28 — High-Volume Pocket Pivot

## Overview
Buy an early accumulation signal when price rises from within a base on volume stronger than recent down days.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock is building a base near 50-day or 10-week MA.
- Up day volume exceeds the largest down-volume day in the prior 10 sessions.
- Price closes strong but has not yet fully broken out.
- Enter near close or next-day hold above pivot low.
- Stop below pivot day low.

## Exit Rules
- Target base breakout or 2R.
- Exit if pivot day low fails.
- Add only if formal breakout follows.

## Filters
- Prefer constructive market environment.
- Avoid stocks below falling 200-day MA.
- Stronger when RS line improves.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-0.75%. Pocket pivots are early; size smaller than full breakouts.
