# 09 — Breakaway Gap Hold

## Overview
Buy a major gap up that holds for several sessions, showing institutions accepted a new valuation zone.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Gap up is caused by earnings, policy, sector catalyst, or corporate action.
- Gap is larger than normal ATR and volume is above 2x average.
- Price holds above gap midpoint for 2-5 sessions.
- Enter on breakout above post-gap high or pullback hold above midpoint.
- Stop below gap midpoint or post-gap low.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R or next weekly resistance.
- Exit if gap midpoint fails on close.
- Trail with 10-day MA after momentum resumes.

## Filters
- Avoid gaps on vague rumors.
- Stronger when sector confirms.
- Skip if stock is already extremely extended on weekly chart.

## Risk Management
Use smaller size after large gaps. Stop distance can be wide.
