# 05 — Sector Supercycle Position

## Overview
Build a position in a sector entering a multi-year investment or earnings cycle.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Sector relative strength breaks a 1-year downtrend.
- Sector fundamentals show durable demand/supply shift.
- Buy the leading stock or sector basket on monthly breakout.
- Add on quarterly pullbacks that hold 10-month MA.

## Exit Rules
- Exit when sector relative strength breaks its uptrend.
- Reduce if commodity/policy/earnings driver reverses.

## Filters
- Avoid late-cycle narratives without earnings confirmation.
- Prefer broad participation across sector leaders.

## Risk Management
Limit total sector exposure to avoid concentration risk.
