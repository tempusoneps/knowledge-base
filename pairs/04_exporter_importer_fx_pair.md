# 04 — Exporter vs Importer FX Pair

## Overview
Trade opposite FX sensitivity by going long exporters and short importers when currency trends create margin divergence.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Identify one exporter benefiting from currency move.
- Identify importer hurt by the same currency move.
- FX trend persists above/below 50-day MA.
- Enter when pair ratio confirms breakout.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if FX trend reverses.
- Take profit when ratio reaches prior weekly resistance.

## Filters
- Verify hedging policies and revenue/cost currency mix.
- Avoid single-name earnings surprises.

## Risk Management
Size by volatility and avoid excessive net market beta.
