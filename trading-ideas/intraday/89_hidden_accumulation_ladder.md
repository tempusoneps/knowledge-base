# 89 — Hidden Accumulation Ladder

## Overview

Trade a slow upside continuation when repeated higher lows form while volume quietly accumulates, but price has not yet made a dramatic breakout. The setup aims to enter before obvious momentum traders arrive.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 5-min chart |
| Markets | Liquid HSX stocks |
| Indicators | VWAP, volume pace, accumulation/distribution optional |

### Long Entry
1. Price stays above VWAP for at least 45 minutes.
2. Pullbacks are shallow and each low is higher.
3. Down-candle volume contracts, while up-candle volume expands modestly.
4. Enter on break of the ladder's most recent swing high.
5. Stop below the last higher low.

### Short Entry
1. Price stays below VWAP for at least 45 minutes.
2. Bounces are shallow and each high is lower.
3. Up-candle volume contracts while down-candle volume expands.
4. Enter on break of the latest swing low.
5. Stop above the last lower high.

## Exit Rules

- Target prior day high/low or 2R.
- Take partials if price accelerates vertically.
- Exit if VWAP is lost after entry.

## Filters

- Avoid if the ladder forms inside a very narrow, fee-sensitive range.
- Best when sector flow is supportive.
- Skip after a late parabolic spike.

## Risk Management

This is a patient trend setup. Do not chase if entry triggers far above the last stop level.
