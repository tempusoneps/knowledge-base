# 05 — Local Currency Devaluation Exporters

## Overview
Buy exporters when local currency weakness improves competitiveness and exporter stocks confirm.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Local currency breaks down versus trade-weighted basket.
- Exporter sector relative strength turns up.
- Enter exporter basket/leader on weekly breakout.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if currency stabilizes and exporter RS fails.
- Stop below sector breakout level.

## Filters
- Confirm exporter costs are not also FX-linked.
- Avoid firms with heavy FX debt.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1.5%. Currency policy can reverse the thesis quickly.
