# 05 — Flat Base Breakout

## Overview
Buy a stock that moves sideways in a shallow range after a prior uptrend, then breaks to new highs.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Prior uptrend exists before the base.
- Base lasts at least 5 weeks and depth is less than 15%.
- Daily closes cluster tightly near resistance.
- Enter on close above base high with volume above average.
- Stop below breakout day low or base midpoint.

## Exit Rules
- Target 2R-3R or measured move equal to base depth.
- Exit if breakout fails back into base.
- Trail below 20-day MA once profitable.

## Filters
- Prefer bases above rising 50-day MA.
- Avoid bases with repeated high-volume distribution days.
- Confirm market index is constructive.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Tight bases allow better position sizing than wide bases.
