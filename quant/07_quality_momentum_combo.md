# 07 — Quality Momentum Combo

## Overview
Combine quality factors with price momentum to avoid low-quality momentum traps.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Rank quality: ROE/ROIC, margins, leverage, earnings stability.
- Rank momentum: 6-12 month relative return.
- Buy stocks scoring high on both.
- Rebalance monthly/quarterly.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if combined rank falls below median.
- Stop if price loses 200-day MA.

## Filters
- Use sector-relative scores.
- Remove stocks with stale or missing fundamentals.

## Risk Management
Cap single-name and sector weights; rebalance systematically.
