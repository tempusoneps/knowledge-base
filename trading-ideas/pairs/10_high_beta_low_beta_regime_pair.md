# 10 — High Beta vs Low Beta Regime Pair

## Overview
Trade market-risk appetite by pairing high-beta stocks against low-beta stocks.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Risk-on: market breadth improves and volatility falls.
- Long high-beta basket, short low-beta basket.
- Risk-off: reverse the pair.
- Enter when high/low beta ratio breaks a 20-day range.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if volatility regime flips.
- Stop if ratio returns inside range.

## Filters
- Use diversified baskets, not one stock each.
- Avoid earnings-heavy windows.

## Risk Management
Keep net beta near intended exposure. Basket construction matters more than prediction.
