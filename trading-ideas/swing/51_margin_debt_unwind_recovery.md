# 51 — Margin Unwind Recovery

## Overview
Buy a quality stock after forced margin selling appears exhausted and price reclaims a key level.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock experiences sharp forced-looking decline on heavy volume.
- News/fundamentals do not justify permanent impairment.
- Price stabilizes for at least 5 sessions.
- Enter on reclaim of 10-day/20-day MA or range high.
- Stop below forced-selling low.

## Exit Rules
- Target 50-day MA or prior breakdown level.
- Exit if forced-selling low fails.
- Take partial at 2R.

## Filters
- Avoid companies with governance or solvency issues.
- Stronger during market-wide liquidation recovery.
- Require volume normalization.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%. Forced selling can resume; keep size conservative.
