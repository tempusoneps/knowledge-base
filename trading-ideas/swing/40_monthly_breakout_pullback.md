# 40 — Monthly Breakout Pullback

## Overview
Buy a stock after it breaks a multi-month or multi-year resistance and retests the breakout zone.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Monthly chart shows clear resistance.
- Price closes above resistance on monthly or weekly basis.
- Pullback retests the breakout zone without heavy selling.
- Enter on daily/weekly reversal from the zone.
- Stop below retest low or monthly breakout level.

## Exit Rules
- Target next monthly resistance or 3R.
- Exit if price closes back below breakout zone on weekly basis.
- Trail with 10-week MA for large trends.

## Filters
- Prefer improving fundamentals or sector cycle.
- Avoid false breakouts in weak markets.
- Use adjusted long-term charts.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Monthly setups can be powerful but require patience and smaller sizing.
