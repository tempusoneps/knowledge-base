# 32 — Low-Volume Node Launch

## Overview
Buy a breakout from value into a low-volume area where price can move quickly due to limited overhead supply.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Multi-week volume profile shows low-volume node above current base.
- Price consolidates below the low-volume area.
- Breakout closes above base resistance with volume.
- Enter on breakout or retest of base top.
- Stop below breakout level.

## Exit Rules
- Target next high-volume node.
- Exit if price falls back into base.
- Trail if move accelerates through thin volume.

## Filters
- Avoid if daily liquidity is too low.
- Stronger when market and sector confirm.
- Do not use on distorted volume data.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Thin zones can reverse sharply at the next value node.
