# 96 — Intraday Beta Chase

## Overview

When the index breaks into a strong trend, high-beta stocks often move later but faster. This setup buys or shorts high-beta names only after the index trend is confirmed and the stock begins to respond.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 5-min chart |
| Markets | High-beta liquid stocks |
| Benchmark | VNINDEX or VN30 |

### Long Entry
1. Index breaks HOD and holds above rising VWAP.
2. High-beta stock is above VWAP but has not extended as much as the index.
3. Stock relative volume rises.
4. Enter when stock breaks its own 5-min range high.
5. Stop below VWAP or the range low.

### Short / Sell Entry
1. Index breaks LOD and holds below falling VWAP.
2. High-beta stock is below VWAP but has not fully followed.
3. Relative volume rises on selling.
4. Enter short where possible, or sell/avoid, when stock breaks range low.
5. Stop above VWAP or range high.

## Exit Rules

- Target stock HOD/LOD or 2R.
- Exit if index loses trend structure.
- Trail behind 5-min swings during trend acceleration.

## Filters

- Avoid names with independent news against the trade.
- Prefer sectors with broad participation.
- Skip if stock spread widens during index move.

## Risk Management

High-beta names move fast. Use smaller size and pre-defined stop orders.
