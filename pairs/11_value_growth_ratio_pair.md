# 11 — Value vs Growth Ratio Pair

## Overview
Trade style rotation between value and growth baskets.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Build value and growth baskets or use ETFs.
- Ratio breaks 50-day MA with macro support.
- Long outperforming style, short underperforming style.
- Confirm with breadth inside each basket.

## Exit Rules
- Exit on ratio close back below/above 50-day MA.
- Take profit near prior ratio extremes.

## Filters
- Growth often benefits from falling yields; value from rising yields or cyclicals.
- Avoid style signals during earnings distortion.

## Risk Management
Use diversified baskets and volatility targeting.
