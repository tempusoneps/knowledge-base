# 19 — Domestic vs Export Revenue Pair

## Overview
Trade divergence between domestic-demand stocks and export-driven stocks as economic conditions shift.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Domestic indicators or export indicators show a clear trend change.
- Build one domestic-demand stock/basket and one exporter stock/basket.
- Enter when domestic/export ratio breaks a 3-month range.
- Size by volatility.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if macro data reverses or ratio loses breakout.
- Take profit near prior multi-month ratio extreme.

## Filters
- Verify revenue exposure from disclosures.
- Avoid companies with unrelated catalysts.

## Risk Management
Use baskets if single-stock exposures are noisy.
