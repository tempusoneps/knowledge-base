# 47 — Index Inclusion Run-Up

## Overview
Buy likely index additions after official or highly probable inclusion is confirmed by price and volume.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock is announced or strongly expected to enter an index/ETF basket.
- Volume expands and price holds above 20-day MA.
- Pullback remains shallow before effective date.
- Enter on breakout above post-announcement range.
- Stop below range low.

## Exit Rules
- Take partial before effective date.
- Exit if price loses 20-day MA on volume.
- Avoid holding full size into rebalance close unless planned.

## Filters
- Confirm free float and liquidity criteria.
- Avoid if run-up is already excessive.
- Stronger when multiple funds must buy.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%. Event crowding can cause sharp sell-the-news reversals.
