# 03 — Multi-Year Base Breakout

## Overview
Buy a stock breaking out from a base that has lasted one year or longer, often marking a major re-rating.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Base lasts at least 12 months.
- Price compresses near the upper part of the range.
- Monthly close breaks above base resistance with volume.
- Enter on breakout or first monthly/weekly retest.

## Exit Rules
- Initial target is base height projected upward.
- Exit if price closes back inside the base for two weeks.

## Filters
- Prefer improving fundamentals or sector cycle turn.
- Avoid breakouts caused by illiquid one-day spikes.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%. Wide bases require patience and position sizing from the weekly stop.
