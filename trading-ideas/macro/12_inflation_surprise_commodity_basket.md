# 12 — Inflation Surprise Commodity Basket

## Overview
Buy a commodity basket or inflation beneficiaries after repeated upside inflation surprises.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Inflation surprise index or CPI trend beats expectations.
- Commodity basket breaks above 3-month range.
- Enter diversified commodity ETF/futures basket or producers.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if inflation surprises turn negative.
- Stop if commodity basket loses breakout.

## Filters
- Avoid if central-bank tightening crushes demand-sensitive commodities.
- Diversify across commodities.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%. Commodity baskets need volatility targeting.
