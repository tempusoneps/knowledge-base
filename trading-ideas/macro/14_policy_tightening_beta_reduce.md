# 14 — Policy Tightening Beta Reduce

## Overview
Reduce high-beta exposure or short weak beta when policy tightening tightens liquidity.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Central bank hikes or signals sustained tightening.
- Yields and currency funding stress rise.
- High-beta/low-beta ratio breaks down.
- Enter defensive pair or reduce high-beta longs.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if tightening expectations reverse.
- Stop if high-beta ratio reclaims breakdown.

## Filters
- Avoid if earnings growth is strong enough to offset tightening.
- Confirm credit spreads.

## Risk Management
This is a risk overlay. Keep hedge size aligned with portfolio beta.
