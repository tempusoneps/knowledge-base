# 16 — Current Account FX Pressure

## Overview
Trade currency or local equity pressure when current-account trends deteriorate and FX breaks support.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Trade balance/current account weakens for several readings.
- Currency breaks below multi-month support.
- Equity index or import-sensitive sectors underperform.
- Enter FX trend trade or hedge local equity exposure.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if currency reclaims support.
- Remove hedge if current-account data improves.

## Filters
- Watch central-bank intervention risk.
- Confirm with reserve and rate data.

## Risk Management
Use stop levels in FX and avoid oversized equity hedges.
