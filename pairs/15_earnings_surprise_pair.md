# 15 — Earnings Surprise Pair

## Overview
Pair long positive earnings surprise stocks against short negative surprise peers in the same sector.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Same-sector companies report within close window.
- Long company with beat/raise and strong price reaction.
- Short peer with miss/cut or weak reaction.
- Enter when pair ratio breaks post-earnings range.

## Exit Rules
- Exit after drift reaches 2R or before next earnings.
- Stop if ratio reverses through post-earnings range.

## Filters
- Normalize for size and beta.
- Avoid pairs with unrelated business mixes.

## Risk Management
Earnings drift can gap on follow-up news; keep exposure controlled.
