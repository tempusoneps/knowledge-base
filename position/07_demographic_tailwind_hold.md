# 07 — Demographic Tailwind Hold

## Overview
Own companies benefiting from durable demographic shifts, using technical trend rules for timing.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Business has clear demographic demand driver.
- Revenue growth is stable for multiple quarters.
- Price forms higher highs on monthly chart.
- Enter on monthly breakout or 10-month MA pullback.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if growth thesis deteriorates or monthly trend breaks.
- Reduce after valuation becomes extreme vs history.

## Filters
- Prefer companies with pricing power.
- Avoid narratives without measurable revenue impact.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%-1.5%. Position sizing should reflect lower turnover and wider stops.
