# 09 — Sector ETF vs Stock Alpha Pair

## Overview
Long the strongest stock in a sector while shorting the sector ETF/basket to isolate company-level alpha.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Sector is volatile or directionally uncertain.
- Candidate stock has superior earnings/RS.
- Long stock and short sector basket in beta-adjusted size.
- Enter when stock/basket ratio breaks out.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if ratio loses breakout.
- Take profit at 2R or major ratio resistance.

## Filters
- Basket must represent the stock's true sector exposure.
- Avoid single-stock liquidity mismatch.

## Risk Management
Monitor correlation drift and sector concentration.
