# 94 — Analyst Upgrade Gap Hold

## Overview

Trade continuation after an analyst upgrade or target-price raise only if the stock holds the opening gap and shows real buying. Many upgrade gaps fade; this setup focuses on the smaller subset with acceptance.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 5-min chart |
| Markets | Liquid stocks with analyst coverage |
| Context | Upgrade, target increase, positive initiation |

### Long Entry
1. Stock gaps up on confirmed analyst action.
2. First 15-30 minutes hold above PDC and VWAP.
3. Pullback volume is below opening volume.
4. Price forms a higher low above gap midpoint.
5. Enter long on break of the pullback high.

### Failed Upgrade Fade
1. Stock gaps up but immediately loses VWAP.
2. Price breaks below gap midpoint.
3. Enter short where possible, or use as sell signal, on failed VWAP retest.
4. Stop above the failed retest high.

## Exit Rules

- Continuation target: opening high extension or daily resistance.
- Fade target: PDC or gap fill midpoint.
- Exit if price chops around VWAP without direction.

## Filters

- Stronger when upgrade changes consensus materially.
- Avoid low-liquidity coverage notes.
- Check whether the stock already rallied before the report.

## Risk Management

Risk 0.5%-0.75%. Do not hold if the market rejects the catalyst intraday.
