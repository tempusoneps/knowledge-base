# 116 — Afternoon Range Expansion

## Overview

Trade the break of a quiet midday range when the afternoon session reintroduces liquidity. This is tailored to markets with a lunch break or clear afternoon participation shift.

## Setup & Entry Rules

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Timeframe | 5-min chart |
| Markets | VN30F, HSX stocks |
| Context | Post-lunch restart or afternoon liquidity return |

### Long Entry
1. Morning creates a defined range.
2. Midday/early afternoon volatility compresses inside the range.
3. After liquidity returns, price closes above the compression high.
4. Volume pace improves versus lunch period.
5. Enter long on breakout or retest.

### Short Entry
1. Morning range is defined.
2. Afternoon compression forms near range low or below VWAP.
3. Price closes below compression low with volume improvement.
4. Enter short on breakdown or failed retest.
5. Stop inside compression range.

## Exit Rules

- Target morning HOD/LOD or full morning range extension.
- Exit if price returns into compression range.
- Close before ATC if move stalls.

## Filters

- Avoid if morning range is already extremely wide.
- Stronger when afternoon news or sector flow supports direction.
- Skip false breaks with no volume improvement.

## Risk Management

Risk 0.5%-0.75%. Afternoon trades have less time, so require clear R:R.
