# 09 — Deleveraging Re-Rating

## Overview
Hold a company as debt reduction lowers financial risk and valuation multiples expand.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Net debt/EBITDA declines for several quarters.
- Cash flow covers interest and capex.
- Stock reclaims 200-day MA and breaks a long base.
- Enter on breakout or first weekly retest.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if leverage trend reverses.
- Technical exit below 30-week MA.

## Filters
- Avoid firms selling core assets just to survive.
- Prefer improving operating earnings.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%. Balance-sheet trades can gap on credit events.
