# 44 — Commodity Price Momentum Producer

## Overview
Buy producers when their underlying commodity breaks out and equity price confirms leverage to the move.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Commodity breaks a 3-6 month resistance.
- Producer stock forms a base or reclaims 50-day MA.
- Relative strength vs market and commodity peers improves.
- Enter on stock breakout with commodity confirmation.
- Stop below stock base low.

## Exit Rules
- Target weekly resistance or 3R.
- Exit if commodity fails breakout.
- Trail with 20-day or 50-day MA depending on volatility.

## Filters
- Confirm producer benefits from spot price.
- Avoid excessive debt or hedging that limits upside.
- Prefer sector-wide confirmation.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.75%-1%. Commodity equities gap often; keep exposure diversified.
