# 11 — Inflation Beneficiary Hold

## Overview
Hold companies with pricing power or asset exposure during an inflationary regime.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Inflation indicators trend above policy target.
- Company margins remain stable or expand.
- Stock breaks above multi-month resistance.
- Enter on weekly breakout or pullback to 10-week MA.

## Exit Rules
- Exit if inflation trend rolls over and sector loses RS.
- Technical exit on weekly close below 30-week MA.

## Filters
- Prefer real-asset, commodity-linked, or pricing-power firms.
- Avoid companies with rising input costs and no pass-through.

## Risk Management
Risk 1%. Watch rate sensitivity; inflation winners can reverse when policy tightens.
