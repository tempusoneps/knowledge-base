# 70 — Turnaround First Higher Low

## Overview
Buy early turnaround only after the stock forms its first meaningful higher low after a long downtrend.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock has been in a downtrend for months.
- Price capitulates, rebounds, then pulls back.
- Pullback holds above prior major low.
- Enter on break above pullback high.
- Stop below higher low.

## Exit Rules
- Target 50-day or 200-day MA depending on trend maturity.
- Exit if higher low fails.
- Take partial at 2R.

## Filters
- Require evidence that fundamentals are stabilizing.
- Avoid highly levered distressed names.
- Stronger with sector recovery.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%. Early turnarounds fail often; keep entries tight.
