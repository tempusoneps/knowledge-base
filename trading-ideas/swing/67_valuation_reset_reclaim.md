# 67 — Valuation Reset Reclaim

## Overview
Buy after a high-valuation stock resets sharply, bases, and then reclaims a key moving average.

## Setup & Entry Rules
- Stock declines 30%-60% from peak valuation.
- Fundamentals stabilize or revisions stop falling.
- Price builds a base for at least 6 weeks.
- Enter on reclaim of 50-day MA plus base resistance.
- Stop below reclaim low.

## Exit Rules
- Target 200-day MA or prior weekly resistance.
- Exit if base low fails.
- Trail only after 200-day MA reclaim.

## Filters
- Avoid companies with broken business models.
- Stronger when peer group also recovers.
- Require volume confirmation.

## Risk Management
Risk 0.5%-0.75%. Reset trades are recovery trades, not automatic bargains.
